# DynastyMath

Free fantasy football start/sit tool and dynasty trade calculator with superflex, TE premium and picks. Import your Sleeper league. Updated daily.

**Live site: [https://dynastymath.com/](https://dynastymath.com/)** (free, no signup, runs in your browser)

## Tools
- [Start/sit tool](https://dynastymath.com/start-sit/): Type two to four players. Get a start/sit call with confidence.
- [Dynasty trade analyzer](https://dynastymath.com/trade-analyzer/): Is this dynasty trade fair? Players and picks, with balance suggestions.
- [Sleeper league import](https://dynastymath.com/league-import/): Your optimal lineup, league power rankings and best free agents.
- [Waiver wire helper](https://dynastymath.com/waiver-wire/): Who to pick up: trending adds, and free agents who beat your starters.
- [Rookie draft board](https://dynastymath.com/rookie-draft-board/): A live draft board ranked by dynasty value. Tick players off as they go.

## Data sources
Player data, injuries, projections, ADP and trending adds come from the Sleeper public API. Dynasty trade values come from DynastyProcess open data (GPL-3.0), combined with Sleeper and Fantasy Football Calculator dynasty ADP and nflverse production into a composite value. The page "How our values work" explains the weights, and the data refreshes daily.

## About this repo
