Frequently asked questions

Who gained the most dynasty value this week?

Since Oct 24, the biggest 1QB composite value gains were Dak Prescott (+1,755), Omar Cooper Jr. (+1,508), Chase Brown (+1,360), Emeka Egbuka (+1,310), as of October 31, 2026.

Who lost the most dynasty value this week?

Since Oct 24, the biggest losses were Jeremiyah Love (-2,078), Mike Evans (-1,840), Christian Watson (-1,540), Sam LaPorta (-1,067), as of October 31, 2026.

Why did a player’s dynasty value change?

The composite blends expert consensus (updated weekly), dynasty ADP from Sleeper (changes daily) and nflverse production (changes after each game). The page shows how far a number moved, not why. News, role changes and injuries are the usual drivers.

How far back does the comparison go?

It compares today with Oct 24 (7 days ago). Once a full week of history exists, the baseline stays seven days back.
