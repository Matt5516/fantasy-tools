Frequently asked questions

Who gained the most dynasty value this week?

Since Oct 15, the biggest 1QB composite value gains were Jeremiyah Love (+2,165), C.J. Stroud (+1,130), Bo Nix (+1,049), Germie Bernard (+940), as of October 22, 2026.

Who lost the most dynasty value this week?

Since Oct 15, the biggest losses were Davante Adams (-2,236), Rhamondre Stevenson (-2,121), Drake London (-1,157), Matthew Stafford (-1,136), as of October 22, 2026.

Why did a player’s dynasty value change?

The composite blends expert consensus (updated weekly), dynasty ADP from Sleeper (changes daily) and nflverse production (changes after each game). The page shows how far a number moved, not why. News, role changes and injuries are the usual drivers.

How far back does the comparison go?

It compares today with Oct 15 (7 days ago). Once a full week of history exists, the baseline stays seven days back.
