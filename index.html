How the start/sit tool and trade calculator work

Start/sit in one line: start the player with the higher start score: 50% this week's projection (Sleeper/RotoWire), 25% the last three games (nflverse) and 25% season points per game, cut if he's Questionable (×0.85), Doubtful (×0.25) or Out (0).

Trades in one line: a dynasty trade is fair when both sides are within 5% after extra pieces are discounted. One star beats two average starters.

Formulas

Start score = (0.7 × projection + 0.3 × points per game) × injury factor Side value = best piece × 100% + 2nd × 75% + 3rd × 55% + 4th × 40% + 5th × 30% … Verdict = fair if |A − B| ÷ max(A, B) ≤ 5%; otherwise the bigger side wins Superflex = every source on the superflex value curve; TE premium = tight end value × 1.15

Worked example

Team A trades a player worth 8,000. Team B sends two players worth 5,000 and 4,000. The raw totals (8,000 vs 9,000) favor Team B, but after the depth discount Team B's side counts as 5,000 + 0.75 × 4,000 = 8,000. That's even, so the trade is fair. The side getting the single best player usually comes out ahead on roster spots.

Key terms

  • Dynasty league: you keep your whole roster year to year, so age and future picks matter.
  • Superflex: one lineup slot can be a quarterback, which makes QBs far more valuable.
  • TE premium: tight ends get extra points per catch.
  • PPR: one point per reception (Half PPR = 0.5).

Frequently asked questions

Who should I start this week?

Start the player with the higher start score: 50% this week's projection (Sleeper/RotoWire), 25% the last three games (nflverse) and 25% season points per game, reduced for injury status (Questionable × 0.85, Doubtful × 0.25, Out or IR = 0). Check the news before kickoff. Injuries and weather change fast.

How does the dynasty trade calculator value players?

Each value blends four free sources: DynastyProcess expert consensus (40%), Sleeper dynasty ADP (30%), Fantasy Football Calculator dynasty ADP (15%) and nflverse on-field production (15%). Every source is put on the same scale by positional rank. Draft pick values come from DynastyProcess. Sources and licenses are on the values page.

Why does a 2-for-1 trade look worse than the totals suggest?

Roster spots are limited, so one great player is usually worth more than two good ones. The best piece counts in full and extra pieces are discounted (75%, 55%, 40% and so on), the same idea as KeepTradeCut's value adjustment.

What counts as a fair trade?

A trade is fair when the two sides are within 5% of each other after the depth discount. Bigger gaps show who wins and suggest players to balance it.

Does it work for superflex and TE premium leagues?

Yes. Superflex converts every source with the DynastyProcess superflex value curve, which lifts quarterbacks. TE premium adds 15% to tight end values.
