        How the start/sit tool and trade calculator work

        Start/sit in one line: start the player with the higher start score, which is 70% this week's projected fantasy points plus 30% season points per game, reduced if he's Questionable (×0.85), Doubtful (×0.25) or Out (0).

        Trades in one line: a dynasty trade is fair when both sides are within 5% after extra pieces are discounted, because one star is worth more than two average starters.

        Formulas

        Start score = (0.7 × projection + 0.3 × points per game) × injury factor Side value = best piece × 100% + 2nd × 75% + 3rd × 55% + 4th × 40% + 5th × 30% … Verdict = fair if |A − B| ÷ max(A, B) ≤ 5%; otherwise the bigger side wins Superflex = DynastyProcess 2QB values; TE premium = tight end value × 1.15

        Worked example

        Team A trades a player worth 8,000. Team B sends two players worth 5,000 and 4,000. The raw totals (8,000 vs 9,000) favor Team B, but after the depth discount Team B's side counts as 5,000 + 0.75 × 4,000 = 8,000. That's even, so the trade is fair. The team getting the single best player usually comes out ahead on roster spots.

        Key terms

        • Dynasty league: you keep your whole roster year to year, so age and future picks matter.
        • Superflex: one lineup slot can be a quarterback, which makes QBs far more valuable.
        • TE premium: tight ends get extra points per catch.
        • PPR: one point per reception (Half PPR = 0.5).

        Frequently asked questions

        Who should I start this week?

        Start the player with the higher start score: 70% this week's projected points and 30% season points per game, reduced for injury status (Questionable × 0.85, Doubtful × 0.25, Out or IR = 0). Check news before kickoff, since injuries and weather change fast.

        How does the dynasty trade calculator value players?

        Player and draft pick values come from DynastyProcess open data, which is built from expert consensus rankings for 1QB and superflex leagues. Players without a DynastyProcess value use Sleeper dynasty ADP with an age curve. Values run from 0 to about 10,000.

        Why does a 2-for-1 trade look worse than the totals suggest?

        Roster spots are limited, so one great player is usually worth more than two good ones. The calculator counts the best piece in full and discounts extra pieces (75%, 55%, 40% and so on), which is the same idea as KeepTradeCut's value adjustment.

        What counts as a fair trade?

        The calculator calls a trade fair when the two sides are within 5% of each other after the depth discount. Bigger gaps say which side wins and suggest players who would balance it.

        Does it work for superflex and TE premium leagues?

        Yes. Superflex uses DynastyProcess 2QB/superflex values, which lift quarterbacks. TE premium adds 15% to tight end values.

        Can I import my Sleeper league?

        Yes. Enter your Sleeper username, pick a league, and the tool loads your roster, fills your league's starting slots with the highest-scoring players this week and lists your bench. It uses Sleeper's free public API and needs no login or password.

        Where does the data come from and how often is it updated?

        Player info, injuries, stats, projections and ADP come from the free Sleeper public API. Dynasty values come from DynastyProcess (GPL-3.0). A GitHub Action refreshes the data every morning.
