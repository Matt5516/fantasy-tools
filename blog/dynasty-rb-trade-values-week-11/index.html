Dynasty RB Trade Values, Week 11: Age Tiers and Fair Swaps

Dynasty RB trade values for Week 11 as of Nov 20, 2026: backs ranked 11-25 with 1QB and superflex values, changes since Nov 14, age tiers and two fair swaps.

As of Nov 20, 2026, Jahmyr Gibbs (9,347), Bijan Robinson (8,456) and Kenneth Walker III (7,741) lead dynasty running backs on 1QB value. Since Nov 14, Jonah Coleman rose the most among RB11-RB25 (+1,128) and Jaylen Warren fell the most (-1,210).

By DynastyMath Staff · Published November 21, 2026 · Numbers as of November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

The top of the running back board barely moved this week. The action is underneath. Jonah Coleman jumped 1,128 points in six days and Jaylen Warren lost 1,210. Values as of Nov 20, 2026, compared with Nov 14, the day of our Week 10 running back list.

Last week was the stars. This week is the middle class: running backs ranked 11th through 25th, the players most managers are actually shopping. Values run on a 0-10,000 scale. FFC had no dynasty data on Nov 20, so the market side is Sleeper only. The top is quick to sum up: Jahmyr Gibbs sits at 9,347 (-77 since Nov 14), Bijan Robinson at 8,456 (-100) and Kenneth Walker III at 7,741 (-182, listed Questionable). James Cook III is fourth at 7,086.

The risers: Coleman, Skattebo, Hampton

Jonah Coleman is the story. He went from 2,001 to 3,129, a 56.4% gain, and he now ranks RB18. He is 23, scores 12.6 per game and 16 over his last three. Only 5 games are on the books, so the sample is small.

Cam Skattebo added 873 to reach 2,815 (age 24, last 3 at 17.8) and carries a sell flag. Omarion Hampton added 630 to reach 2,039 and carries a buy flag. A sell flag means the draft market sits at least 35% above the experts, a buy flag at least 35% below. Know which one you are paying for.

Chase Brown climbed 469 to 3,311 and averages 18.3 over his last three, against 14.4 for the season. Tony Pollard is up 294 to 1,504. At 29 he posted 29.7 per game over his last three, the best recent average of these twelve.

The fallers: Warren and Hubbard

Jaylen Warren is the sharpest drop in the group: -1,210, from 3,115 to 1,905, or -38.8%. He is 28 and still scores 16.7 per game, with 15.7 over his last three. The price fell harder than the production.

Chuba Hubbard is next at -865 to 3,133. He is 27 and averages 20.1 per game, the best of the twelve, with 24.2 over his last three. That is a price drop with strong scoring behind it, and he carries a sell flag. If you hold him, decide whether the points matter more to you than the value.

The injured: Achane and Hall

De'Von Achane is listed IR and sits at 4,216, up 239. He has played only 3 games and averages 8.2, the lowest PPG of the twelve. Check his status before any offer. Breece Hall (4,181, +290) has played 5 games and carries a buy flag.

The 24-26 group averages 4,749, the highest of the four bands, and holds the top three backs. The 27-29 group is the lowest at 2,757 across 10 backs. That band is a mix. James Cook III is 27 and fourth at 7,086, and Jonathan Taylor is 27 and tenth at 4,566. It also holds D'Andre Swift (4,051), Saquon Barkley (29, 1,882) and Jaylen Warren (28, 1,905).

Only two backs in the top 30 are 30 or older: Christian McCaffrey (30, 5,918, flagged sell) and Derrick Henry (32, 1,844). Christian McCaffrey scores 23.7 per game and 30.3 over his last three, so he is not cheap production. He is a price check, and his value is +73 since Nov 14.

Meanwhile Bucky Irving is up 1,145 to 5,409, the biggest gain in the top 30, and Javonte Williams is up 582 to 5,338. Both carry a sell flag. Jeremiyah Love, age 21 and listed IR, is at 5,554 (-202) on 8.9 per game.

One more name from deeper in the list: David Montgomery is only RB30 at 1,005, but he is up 656 since Nov 14, a 188% jump.
