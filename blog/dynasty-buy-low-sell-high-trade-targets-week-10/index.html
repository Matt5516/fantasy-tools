Dynasty Buy-Low and Sell-High Trade Targets Week 10

Week 10 dynasty buy-low and sell-high trade targets as of Nov 10, 2026: 20 buy flags, 33 sell flags, 5 Nov 4 names dropped, five fair trades.

As of Nov 10, 2026, the top dynasty buy-low targets are Ashton Jeanty (5,747) and James Cook III (5,508), priced 72% and 58% under the experts. The top sell-high names are Christian McCaffrey (5,899) and Tee Higgins (5,554), with the draft market 107% and 91% above. 5 of the 18 names on the Nov 4 list lost their flag.

By DynastyMath Staff · Published November 10, 2026 · Numbers as of November 10, 2026 · 5 min read

Six days ago we named 18 trade targets. 13 still carry a flag today and 5 do not. This Week 10 board shows who stayed, who dropped off, who is new, and five trades the calculator calls fair.

A buy-low is a player the dynasty draft market prices at least 35% under the expert consensus. A sell-high is the reverse, at least 35% over. Only players valued at 1,000 or more count, and today that is 20 buys and 33 sells. Values as of Nov 10, 2026. Fantasy Football Calculator had no dynasty data today, so the market side is Sleeper only. The always-current version lives on the buy-low and sell-high page.

The biggest gainer of the group is Colston Loveland, up 690 to 3,609. The biggest loser is Rashee Rice, down 798 to 3,666. A flag that holds while the value falls is the buy-low case at its strongest, since the price got cheaper while the flag stayed on. A flag that holds while the value rises is a sell that got better.

Colston Loveland is the one to watch: he is up 690 since Nov 4 and still 53% under the experts, with 13.6 PPR points a game over his last three against 9.3 for the season. Ashton Jeanty sits at 5,747 with a 72% discount, and his season average is 15.5. Jaylen Waddle is flagged but listed Out, so there is nothing to buy until that tag clears.

A short Week 10 checklist

  1. Check the format. These are 1QB values. In superflex the quarterbacks jump, as Patrick Mahomes shows.
  2. Read the status column before you pay for a discount.
  3. Look at the last-3 average. A buy with a falling average is a hold, not a bargain.
  4. Compare to Nov 4. A flag that survived a week of price moves is sturdier than a brand new one.
  5. Re-check before you offer. These are Nov 10, 2026 values, and values move every day.
