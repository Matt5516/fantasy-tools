Dynasty Draft Pick Trade Values Week 10: Pick Prices vs Players

Week 10 dynasty draft pick trade values: a 2027 early 1st is 4,386 in 1QB and 5,220 in superflex, unchanged since Nov 6. See 14 fair pick-for-player deals.

As of Nov 12, 2026, a 2027 early 1st is 4,386 in 1QB and 5,220 in superflex, the same as Nov 6. The players around it moved: 33 now sit above it in 1QB (35 on Nov 6) and 24 in superflex (23). Bryce Young climbed from 1,299 to 2,435 in 1QB, past the mid 1st (1,985).

By DynastyMath Staff · Published November 12, 2026 · Numbers as of November 12, 2026 · 6 min read

Pick prices held still this week. The players you swap them for did not. Here is what each pick costs on Nov 12, 2026 (week 10), who moved across the pick lines since Nov 6, and the pick-for-player deals the calculator calls fair. Sleeper dynasty drafts supply the draft market. Fantasy Football Calculator had no dynasty data today.

Value you can see move. Jordyn Tyson (WR, 22) cost 2,693 on Nov 6, more than a mid 1st. At 1,616 he is now a 2028 1st in 1QB (1,605 vs 1,616). He was the second-biggest faller in our data. A pick holds its price while the player it buys gets cheaper, so check the date on any quote.

The age flag. Derrick Henry (RB, 32) is 1,911, a mid 1st within 3.7%. A pick does not age and a 32-year-old running back does. Price him as a rental.

The superflex quarterback. Jayden Daniels (QB, 25) is 6,544 in superflex, within 1.4% of an early 1st plus a 2028 1st (6,636). Patrick Mahomes (QB, 31) is 7,077 against an early 1st plus a mid 1st. Both are fair, and both only make sense in a format that starts two quarterbacks.

Going the other way: split a pick

Two starters for one pick is the depth play. In 1QB, Colston Loveland (TE, 22, 3,609) plus Caleb Douglas (WR, 23, 1,035) prices at 4,385 against 4,386 for the early 1st, a gap of 0.0%. In superflex, Colston Loveland (TE, 22, 2,667) plus Chuba Hubbard (RB, 27, 3,192) prices at 5,192 against 5,220 for the early 1st, a gap of 0.5%. How to use this

  1. Name the slot and the year. Early, mid, late and 2028 are four different prices.
  2. Quote your format. Superflex numbers belong in superflex leagues.
  3. Check the date on the player. A pick's price held this week. 1,291 points came off one running back's value in six days.
  4. Count the players above it. Fewer players above a pick means it is worth more as a ranking, not just as a number.
  5. Price the exact deal. Run it through the dynasty trade analyzer, browse the rookie pick values and scout the class on the rookie draft board.

Frequently asked questions

What is a 2027 early 1st worth in dynasty right now?

As of Nov 12, 2026, a 2027 early 1st is 4,386 in 1QB and 5,220 in superflex. A mid 1st is 1,985 and 2,317. A late 1st is 928 and 1,086.

Did dynasty pick values change since last week?

The pick prices did not. Every pick matches its Nov 6 value. The number of players priced above the early 1st went from 35 to 33 in 1QB and from 23 to 24 in superflex.

What can an early 1st buy in 1QB?

Bucky Irving (RB, 24) at 4,372 and Brock Purdy (QB, 26) at 4,318 both price within 2% of the 4,386 pick. Irving was above the pick on Nov 6.

How much is a pick worth in superflex?

More. The four firsts run from +17% (mid and late 2027 1sts) to +19% (early 2027 1st). The early 1st is 5,220 in superflex against 4,386 in 1QB.

Is a 2028 1st worth more than a late 2027 1st?

Yes. A 2028 1st is 1,605 in 1QB and 1,888 in superflex, against 928 and 1,086 for a late 2027 1st. It is below a mid 2027 1st (1,985 and 2,317).
