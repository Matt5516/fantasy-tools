Rest-of-Season Rankings: Most Projected PPR Points, Weeks 12-14

Rest-of-season PPR rankings for Weeks 12-14 as of Nov 26, 2026: Jahmyr Gibbs leads at 80.1 projected points, Jaxon Smith-Njigba tops receivers at 76.2.

As of Nov 26, 2026, Jahmyr Gibbs has the most projected PPR points over Weeks 12-14 among running backs, receivers and tight ends with all three weeks projected (80.1). Jaxon Smith-Njigba leads receivers at 76.2 and is listed Questionable. Josh Allen leads quarterbacks at 67.1 and Isaiah Likely leads tight ends at 41.4.

By DynastyMath Staff · Published November 27, 2026 · Numbers as of November 26, 2026 · 5 min read

Three weeks is the window that matters now. This is the board for Weeks 12, 13 and 14, with PPR projections added up and set beside what each player has scored. Numbers as of Nov 26, 2026. Your league's playoff dates are a league setting, so check them before you lean on a 3-week window.

How to read it. The 3-week total is the sum of the weekly projections, and per week is that total divided by three. ROS score blends the average projection (40%), last-3 PPG (30%) and season PPG (30%). The position tables only include players with all three weeks projected and no Out or IR tag. Players with two projected weeks are in their own table further down, because their totals are not comparable.
