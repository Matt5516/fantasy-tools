Superflex QB Trade Values Week 9: Who Rose and Who Fell

Superflex QB trade values for Week 9 as of Nov 7, 2026: Jared Goff +1,710, Bryce Young -1,574 since Oct 27, plus 10 fair trades.

As of Nov 7, 2026, Josh Allen is still the top superflex asset at 9,662 (6,564 in 1QB). Since Oct 27, Jared Goff gained the most among QBs worth 1,000 or more (+1,710 to 5,581) and Bryce Young lost the most (-1,574 to 3,358). 5 of the top 12 superflex assets are quarterbacks; in 1QB, 1 is.

By DynastyMath Staff · Published November 7, 2026 · Numbers as of November 7, 2026 · 6 min read

The quarterback market moved again. Some prices jumped, some dropped, and the Week 8 fair trades no longer match. Here is the Week 9 reset. Values use a 0 to 10,000 scale, as of Nov 7, 2026. Changes compare to Oct 27, the date of last week's price list. 1QB value is the price in a one-QB league. Superflex value is the price when a second QB can start.

Josh Allen is still the top asset in superflex at 9,662, ahead of Jaxon Smith-Njigba (9,249), the best non-QB. Allen is down 570 since Oct 27 (from 10,232) and still leads by 413. Joe Burrow sits third overall at 8,429, then Brock Purdy at 8,098.

Jared Goff made the biggest jump among the top 12 QBs: 42 in superflex on Oct 27, 22 today, at 5,581. Brock Purdy went from 15 to 6, and Patrick Mahomes is up 1,537 to 7,650.

Lamar Jackson and Jalen Hurts are both listed IR. Jackson's superflex value is down 103 since Oct 27, from 7,898 to 7,795, and Hurts is at 6,076. That is a status tag, not a forecast. Check it before you pay full price.

Among quarterbacks worth at least 1,000 in superflex on Oct 27, Jared Goff is the biggest riser at +1,710 (+44.2%). His 1QB value moved +1,265. Patrick Mahomes is next at +1,537, and Brock Purdy adds +1,365. Bryce Young is the biggest faller at -1,574 (-31.9%), and C.J. Stroud is right behind him at -1,487.

Drew Lock climbed from 3 to 1,812, the largest raw gain of any QB, but he started near zero, so he sits outside this list. By percentage, Malik Willis is the biggest riser on the list (+75.6%) and Geno Smith the biggest faller (-73.7%).

Our data does not say why. A riser can be a sell. A faller can be a buy. The price is the point. If you held C.J. Stroud on Oct 27, you held 5,950 of superflex value. Today it is 4,463.

How to use this

  1. Quote the right column. Use the superflex number in superflex leagues and the 1QB number in 1QB leagues.
  2. Check the change since Oct 27. A QB up 1,710 is priced differently than he was on Oct 27. Counter with today's number.
  3. Check the tag. Status is a label in our data, not a timeline.
  4. Run the deal. The trade analyzer uses the same rule as the tables above.
