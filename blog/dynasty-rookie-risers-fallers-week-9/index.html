Dynasty Rookie Risers and Fallers Week 9: Who Moved Since Oct 27

Week 9 dynasty rookie risers and fallers since Oct 27: Jeremiyah Love +1,385, Jonah Coleman +1,175, Jadarian Price -420, plus market gaps.

As of Nov 3, 2026, Jeremiyah Love is the top 2026 rookie at 5,641 in 1QB leagues, up 1,385 since Oct 27 and past Carnell Tate (3,979). Jonah Coleman is up 1,175 to 1,827. The biggest drop is Jadarian Price, down 420 to 958.

By DynastyMath Staff · Published November 3, 2026 · Numbers as of November 3, 2026 · 6 min read

Love took the top spot. A week ago Carnell Tate sat first at 4,296 and Love second at 4,256. Now it is Love at 5,641 and Tate at 3,979. Further down, Jonah Coleman nearly tripled. Jadarian Price lost almost a third of his price.

This is the Week 9 board. Values are on a 0 to 10,000 scale as of Nov 3, 2026, and every change compares to Oct 27. 87 first-year players carry a value. Of the 86 that had one a week ago, 38 gained, 26 lost and 22 did not move. Full class: dynasty rookie rankings. The lists below skip rookies worth less than 250 on both dates, so one-point wiggles do not crowd out the real moves.

Jeremiyah Love (ARI). Up 1,385 (+32.5%) to 5,641, the largest gain in the class by points. He has played 6 games at 8.9 PPR points per game, with 3.7 over his last three, and he is listed Out. Experts value him at 7,312. The production-based number is 1,186. That gap is why he is a hold-and-watch, not a pure buy.

Jonah Coleman (DEN). Up 1,175 (+180.2%) to 1,827, the biggest percentage gain among rookies worth 250 or more. Four games, 11 PPG, 14.7 over the last three. Experts have him at 639. The Sleeper draft market has him at 3,359, so the market is paying far more than the experts are.

Mike Washington Jr. (LV). Up 179 (+135.6%) to 311. That is still a small price. He has played 8 games and scores 4.7 per game, so the climb is a price move, not a production story.

Germie Bernard (PIT). Up 234 to 1,251. His last three average 13.1 PPG against 8.4 for the season. The experts are at 292 and the market at 2,104.

KC Concepcion Jr. (CLE). Up 134 to 1,673 with 12.6 PPG over his last three. Experts sit at 2,119, the market at 1,183.

Chris Bell and Emmett Johnson. Bell is up 82 to 476 on 4.2 PPG. Johnson is up 70 to 260, scoring 7.3 per game with 11.1 over his last three. Both are bench-stash prices, not starter prices.

What to do with this

  1. Sell the market-driven jump. If a manager pays Coleman's 1,827, you are selling at the draft market's price, not the experts'.
  2. Check the tag first. Love and Tate are both listed Out. Price that into any offer. Our data has no return dates.
  3. Price a pick against a rookie. A 2027 Early 1st is 4,178 in 1QB leagues (4,923 superflex) and a 2027 Mid 1st is 1,869 (2,167). Run both through the trade analyzer.
  4. Rank before you offer. Build your list on the rookie draft board so you know your price before the other manager names theirs.
