Dynasty Buy-Low and Sell-High Trade Targets, Week 11

Week 11 dynasty buy-low and sell-high trade targets as of Nov 17, 2026: 16 buy flags, 30 sell flags and five trades the calculator calls fair.

As of Nov 17, 2026, the top dynasty buy-low targets are Ashton Jeanty (5,286) and Jonathan Taylor (4,521), priced 80% and 61% under the experts. The top sell-high names are Tee Higgins (6,244) and Christian McCaffrey (5,614), with the draft market 126% and 70% above. 13 of the 21 names on last week's list still carry their flag.

By DynastyMath Staff · Published November 17, 2026 · Numbers as of November 17, 2026 · 6 min read

Last week's list had 21 names. 13 still carry a flag today and 8 do not. This Week 11 board shows the best buys, the best sells, what moved in seven days, and five trades the calculator calls fair.

A buy-low is a player the dynasty draft market prices at least 35% under the expert consensus. A sell-high is the reverse, at least 35% over. Only players valued at 1,000 or more count, and today that is 16 buys and 30 sells. Values as of Nov 17, 2026. Fantasy Football Calculator had no dynasty data today, so the market side is Sleeper only. Change is measured against Nov 10. The always-current version lives on the buy-low and sell-high page.

A short Week 11 checklist

  1. Check the format. These are 1QB values. In superflex the quarterbacks jump: Brock Purdy goes from 2,828 to 5,852.
  2. Read the status column before you pay for a discount.
  3. Look at the last-3 average. A buy with a falling average is a hold, not a bargain.
  4. Compare to Nov 10. A flag that survived a week of price moves is sturdier than a brand new one.
  5. Re-check before you offer. These are Nov 17, 2026 values, and values move every day. Open the league import tool to see which of these names sit on your roster.
