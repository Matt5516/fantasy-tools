Dynasty RB Trade Values, Week 12: Risers, Fallers and Swaps

Dynasty RB trade values for Week 12 as of Nov 27, 2026: the top 10 backs, the five biggest risers and fallers since Nov 20, and two fair swaps.

As of Nov 27, 2026, Jahmyr Gibbs (9,391), Bijan Robinson (8,555) and Kenneth Walker III (8,012) lead dynasty running backs on 1QB value. Since Nov 20, Emmett Johnson rose the most in the top 40 (+2,604) and D'Andre Swift fell the most (-1,393).

By DynastyMath Staff · Published November 28, 2026 · Numbers as of November 27, 2026 · 5 min read

Emmett Johnson is the story of Week 12. He went from RB36 to RB15, up 2,604 points to 3,393. D'Andre Swift went the other way and lost 1,393. Values as of Nov 27, 2026, compared with Nov 20, the day of our Week 11 running back list.

Last week was the middle class. This week we track the whole top 40 and ask what moved in seven days. The top four held their ranks: Jahmyr Gibbs (9,391), Bijan Robinson (8,555), Kenneth Walker III (8,012) and James Cook III (6,576). Walker is listed Out, so check his status before any offer. Values run on a 0-10,000 scale. FFC had no dynasty data on Nov 27, so the market side is Sleeper only.

Emmett Johnson, a 21-spot jump

Emmett Johnson (KC, age 23) rose 2,604 points, from 789 to 3,393, the largest gain of any back in the top 40 and the only one above 2,000. He scores 12.7 per game and 26.3 over his last three, the best recent average of the five risers. He carries a sell flag, and the draft market is 2046% above the experts. A jump this big is a sell window if you hold him, and a price check if you want him.

Barkley and Coleman, riding the wave

Saquon Barkley added 1,144 to 3,026 and moved from RB23 to RB19. He is 29, listed Questionable, and scores 8.1 per game, the lowest of the five risers. The price is running ahead of the points. Jonah Coleman is up 953 to 4,082 and now ranks RB12. He averages 16.3 and 24.7 over his last three, with 6 games played. That is a small sample.

Swift leads the fallers

D'Andre Swift fell 1,393 to 2,658, from RB13 to RB21, the biggest rank drop in the top 40. He is 27 and averages 17.7 per game with 14.7 over his last three. The score is steady and the price is not. Kyren Williams slipped 658 to 3,296, and James Cook III is down 510 at the top of the board. Check each one against the dynasty RB rankings before you buy the dip.
