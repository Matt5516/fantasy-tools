Week 11 Waiver Wire Pickups: Which Adds the Projections Back

11 of the 25 most-added Week 11 waiver players have a projection of 8 or more. See who to chase for the stretch run, who to skip and the split by position.

Of the 25 most-added players on Sleeper heading into Week 11, 11 have a Week 11 PPR projection of 8 or more, led by Fernando Mendoza (12), Jordyn Tyson (11.4) and Emmett Johnson (10.7). 8 are hype adds with a projection under 6 or none, led by Chimere Dike (413K adds, 5 projected). As of Nov 16, 2026.

By DynastyMath Staff · Published November 16, 2026 · Numbers as of November 16, 2026 · 6 min read

Everybody adds the same names every week, and plenty of them do nothing for you on Sunday. With the stretch run here, a wasted claim costs more. Here is the cut: take the 25 most-added players on Sleeper in the last 24 hours and set each one against his Week 11 projection, his projection for the next three weeks, his season PPG and his last three games. Scoring is PPR. Adds and projections as of Nov 16, 2026.

The rule is simple. Backed means a Week 11 projection of 8 points or more. Hype means a projection under 6, or no projection at all. The 6 in between are depth. Only one of the 25 carries a status tag: Xavier Hutchinson, listed Questionable. These are adds and projections only. They are not a read on role or news.

"Next 3 wks" is the sum of the projections we have, and the number in parentheses is how many weeks that covers. 11 of the 25 clear the bar, and together they pulled 1.64M adds in 24 hours.

Start with the rookies. Fernando Mendoza has the top Week 11 projection on the list, 12, but he is a quarterback with 2 games played and 8.1 PPG. His next-three sum is 25.4 over 2 weeks. In a one-quarterback league that is a stash, not a plan. Jordyn Tyson is the cleaner add. He is a rookie projected at 11.4, with the biggest next-three sum on the whole list, 34.7, and he averages 12 PPG over 2 games. That is a short sample.

Emmett Johnson is the most interesting back. He is a rookie projected at 10.7 with 389K adds in 24 hours and 2.52M in 72, the most over three days. He is averaging 10.7 PPG on the season and 23.3 over his last three, so the recent scoring is running well ahead of the season line. He is the only running back on the list who clears the bar.

The tight ends. Mike Gesicki is projected at 10.4, with 29.6 over the next three weeks. Darren Waller is at 8.8 and 25.8. Michael Mayer is at 8.4 and Brenton Strange is at 9.2. 4 of the 7 tight ends on the list are backed. Ryan Flournoy is the wide receiver to know: 10 projected, 13.2 over his last three and 139K adds.

The loudest names. Chimere Dike has 413K adds in 24 hours, the most of any hype add and second on the whole list. Missing projections. 4 of the 25 have no Week 11 projection in our data: Tutu Atwell, Tyler Higbee, Deshaun Watson and Konata Mumpfield. We won't guess a number for them. Deshaun Watson has 31.6 projected over the next 2 weeks, but that is two weeks of data, and a second quarterback only helps in superflex. Tyler Higbee has 169K adds and no Week 11 projection, with 17 over the next 2 weeks.

The middle: depth, not a plan

6 more are projected between 6 and 8 points, with 1.19M adds between them. Justice Hill is the most-added player on the whole list, 418K in 24 hours and 728K in 72, at 7 projected and 13.9 over 2 weeks. He averages 6.8 PPG on the season and 10.7 over his last three. Quentin Johnston (7.4), Seth McGowan (6.1), Chris Rodriguez Jr. (6.1), Darnell Washington (6.7) and Tank Dell (6.3) fill out the group. Add one if you have a hole and nobody better.

How to use this

  1. Open with the backed list. If one fits your roster hole, bid on him. A playoff push rewards the player who can score now.
  2. Match the add to your need. A second quarterback does nothing in a one-quarterback league.
  3. Skip anyone whose projection is under 6. A heavy add count with a low projection is the pattern to avoid.
  4. Check status before kickoff. These numbers are from Monday. A status change beats them.
  5. Know your own deadlines. Your league's waiver and trade deadlines are league settings. Check them before you plan the stretch run.
  6. Go past the top 25. The full Week 11 waiver wire updates daily, and the waiver wire tool sorts it against your own roster. For the weeks ahead, see the rest-of-season rankings.

Frequently asked questions

Who are the best Week 11 waiver wire pickups?

By projection among the most-added players: Fernando Mendoza (12), Jordyn Tyson (11.4), Emmett Johnson (10.7), Mike Gesicki (10.4). As of Nov 16, 2026.

Which Week 11 waiver adds should I skip?

Skip the hype adds: Chimere Dike (413K adds, 5 projected), Gavin Bartholomew (2.2) and anyone else who pairs heavy adds with a projection under 6 or none.

Is there a good running back on the Week 11 waiver wire?

Not many among the most-added. 5 running backs made the list and only Emmett Johnson (10.7) reaches 8.

Should I add a second quarterback in Week 11?

Only in superflex or a two-quarterback league. Fernando Mendoza has the top projection at 12, but he has 2 games played. In a one-quarterback league one is enough.

Is the Week 11 waiver wire still worth checking for playoff teams?

Yes, if you check the projection and not the add count. For the next-three-week sum, Jordyn Tyson leads this list at 34.7.
