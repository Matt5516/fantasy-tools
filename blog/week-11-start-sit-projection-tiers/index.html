Week 11 Start/Sit Projection Tiers: QB, RB, WR and TE

Week 11 start/sit tiers for QB, RB, WR and TE on PPR projections, with last-3 PPG, 9 Out players still projected and 5 flex ties.

As of Nov 17, 2026, the top healthy Week 11 PPR projections are Josh Allen (25.2), Joe Burrow (25.1) and Jahmyr Gibbs (23.7). CeeDee Lamb (22.2) leads the receivers and Isaiah Likely (15.1) the tight ends. Kenneth Walker III still shows 18.4 but is listed Out. 5 flex groups tie on projection.

By DynastyMath Staff · Published November 17, 2026 · Numbers as of November 17, 2026 · 6 min read

Start the high number. Break ties with recent scoring. Every projection here is PPR points for Week 11, as of Nov 17, 2026, and status is the tag exactly as listed. One trap first: 9 players listed Out still carry a projection of 10 or more, and Kenneth Walker III leads them at 18.4. They are left out of the tiers.

How the tiers work. Inside each position, Tier 1 is any healthy player less than 3 points under the top projection. Tier 2 is 3 to 6 back and Tier 3 is 6 to 9. Players in a tier are close enough to swap, so fill Tier 1 before anything else. Rows below show the top of each position, not the whole list.

  2. Fill flex from the highest projection. Break ties on last-three scoring.

Frequently asked questions

Who are the top fantasy quarterbacks to start in Week 11?

By PPR projection as of Nov 17, 2026: Josh Allen (25.2), Joe Burrow (25.1), Patrick Mahomes (22.6), Dak Prescott (21.9).
