Dynasty WR Trade Values by Age: Sell 27+, Buy Under 25

Dynasty WR trade values by age as of Oct 23, 2026: six receivers 27+ to sell, six 24 and under to buy, with value changes, PPG and five fair trades.

As of Oct 23, 2026, the eight top-24 dynasty receivers aged 27+ are worth 45,957 points combined; the eight aged 24 and under are worth 43,714. Sell Christian Watson (6,329) and Nico Collins; buy Malik Nabers (6,449, age 23) and Carnell Tate (age 21).

By DynastyMath Staff · Published October 24, 2026 · Numbers as of October 23, 2026 · 5 min read

Receivers age slower than running backs, so the sell window is wider. It is still open. Values as of Oct 23, 2026.

Of the top 24 receivers by value, 8 are 27 or older and 8 are 24 or younger. The old group is worth 45,957 points combined. The young group is worth 43,714. That is a gap of 2,243, small enough that the veterans are still priced like stars. The best receiver on the board, Jaxon Smith-Njigba, is 24 and sits at 10,119.

Below: the veterans to sell, the young receivers to buy, and five trades that land within 5% on our calculator rule. Every value is on a 0-10,000 scale. "Flag" is our buy/sell signal: how far the draft market sits from the experts. FFC had no dynasty data today, so the draft market here is Sleeper only.

Frequently asked questions

At what age do dynasty wide receivers lose value?

In our data, 8 of the top 24 receivers are 27 or older, and the group is worth 45,957 points against 43,714 for the 24-and-under group. The gap is small, so receivers hold value longer than running backs.

Which wide receivers should I sell in dynasty right now?

As of Oct 23, 2026: CeeDee Lamb (8,507), Justin Jefferson (7,312), Nico Collins (7,077), Christian Watson (6,329), Tee Higgins (4,194), Jaylen Waddle (3,708). All are 27 or older. Nico Collins, Christian Watson and Tee Higgins carry our sell flag.

Which young wide receivers are dynasty buys?

Malik Nabers (23), Carnell Tate (21) and Emeka Egbuka (24) carry our buy flag, meaning the draft market is at least 35% below the experts. Tetairoa McMillan has no flag.
