Dynasty Draft Pick Trade Values, Week 11: Pick Prices vs Players

Week 11 dynasty draft pick trade values: a 2027 early 1st is 4,456 in 1QB and 5,274 in superflex, up since Nov 12. See nine fair pick-for-player deals.

As of Nov 18, 2026, a 2027 early 1st is 4,456 in 1QB and 5,274 in superflex, up 70 and 54 since Nov 12. The mid 1st is 2,024 and 2,342, the late 1st 948 and 1,099. James Cook III jumped from 5,606 to 7,101 in 1QB.

By DynastyMath Staff · Published November 19, 2026 · Numbers as of November 18, 2026 · 7 min read

A pick does not stand still when the week does. Every pick we price went up since Nov 12, and the players you trade them for moved a lot more. Here is what a 2027 early, mid and late 1st costs on Nov 18, 2026 (week 11), who sits closest to each price, and the pick-for-player deals the calculator calls fair. Sleeper drafts supply the draft market. FFC had no dynasty data on Nov 18.

Picks and players share one 0 to 10,000 scale, so a pick converts straight into a player. Superflex has its own set of numbers. Values in this article are as of Nov 18, 2026, and the change columns compare to Nov 12.

How to use this

  1. Name the slot and the year. Early, mid, late and 2028 are four different prices.
  2. Quote your format. Superflex numbers belong in superflex leagues.
  3. Check the date on the player. The picks rose about 2% in six days. One running back rose 1,495 points in the same span.
  4. Count the players above it. The early 1st has 33 players above it in 1QB. Fewer above a pick means it carries more weight.
  5. Price the exact deal. Run it through the dynasty trade analyzer, browse the rookie pick values, check the trade value chart and scout the class on the rookie draft board.
