Week 9 Fantasy Injury Report: Projections for Banged-Up Starters

Week 9 fantasy injury report as of Nov 5, 2026: PPR projections for 8 of 56 Questionable players, plus Out and IR names and healthy fill-ins within 1 point.

As of Nov 5, 2026, 56 players are listed Questionable, 1 Doubtful, 16 Out, 84 on IR, 4 PUP and 1 DNR. Christian McCaffrey has the top Questionable Week 9 projection at 22.7 PPR, ahead of Amon-Ra St. Brown (20.3) and Drew Lock (17.3).

By DynastyMath Staff · Published November 5, 2026 · Numbers as of November 5, 2026 · 5 min read

Questionable is a label. The projection is the number. Before you bench a starter, see what Week 9 projects for that player and who you can slot in at the same spot. All points are PPR, as of Nov 5, 2026.

Right now 56 players are listed Questionable, 1 Doubtful, 16 Out, 84 on IR, 4 PUP and 1 DNR. We use only that listed status plus the projection, the season average, the last-3 average and the Weeks 9-11 total. No timelines, no causes, no calls on who plays. Christian McCaffrey owns the top Questionable projection at 22.7. The season average is 20.6 and the last-3 average is 23.3, so the projection sits above the season line and just under the recent one. Drew Lock shows 17.3 for Week 9 but nothing past it, which is why that sum carries a star. One Questionable player, Deshaun Watson, has no Week 9 projection at all. That is a missing number, not a zero.

Two names stand apart from their box scores. A.J. Brown is projected 13.2 against 6.4 per game this season, a gap of +6.8 and the widest among Questionable players with a projection. Bhayshul Tuten runs the other way: 14 projected after 22.1 per game over the last three games (-8.1). Treat both as projections, not promises.

Before kickoff

  1. Find every tagged player on your roster in the first two tables. The tag does not change the projection.
  2. Pick the healthy player closest to that number, then compare last 3 PPG and the sum.
  3. If your player has no projection, you are working from averages only.
  4. Put both lineups through the start/sit tool before the first game.
  5. Re-check the injury report before lineups lock. Status moves after Nov 5, 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Which Questionable players have the highest Week 9 projections?

As of Nov 5, 2026: Christian McCaffrey (22.7), Amon-Ra St. Brown (20.3), Drew Lock (17.3), Kyler Murray (17.2) and Mike Evans (16.6), all in PPR points.

Does a Questionable tag mean a player is out?

No. The tag is a status label, not a projection. In our data, 20 Questionable players carry a Week 9 projection of 10 or more points. Check the injury report before lineups lock.

Who is a healthy fill-in for Christian McCaffrey?

Bijan Robinson (23.2) and Kenneth Walker III (22) are within 1.0 point of the 22.7 projected for Christian McCaffrey and have no injury tag in our data.

Why does an Out or IR player show no projection?

Most do not have one. All 16 players listed Out and 81 of 84 listed IR have no Week 9 projection, so there is nothing to compare. The 5 exceptions are on IR or PUP and none is above 7.

Where do I check the latest status?

Check the injury report again right before lineups lock. Tags can change after Nov 5, 2026, so verify before kickoff.
