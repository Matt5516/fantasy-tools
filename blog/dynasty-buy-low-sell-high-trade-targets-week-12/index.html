Dynasty Buy-Low and Sell-High Trade Targets, Week 12

Week 12 dynasty buy-low and sell-high trade targets as of Nov 24, 2026: 15 buy flags, 35 sell flags and five trades the calculator calls fair.

As of Nov 24, 2026, the top dynasty buy-low targets are James Cook III (5,692) and Josh Allen (5,517), priced 45% and 41% under the experts. The top sell-high names are Nico Collins (6,645) and Tee Higgins (6,456), with the draft market 36% and 119% above. 12 of the 16 names on last week's tables still carry a flag.

By DynastyMath Staff · Published November 24, 2026 · Numbers as of November 24, 2026 · 6 min read

Cook is back on the buy board and Collins leads the sells. Of the 16 names in last week's tables, 12 still carry a flag and 4 do not. This Week 12 board shows the best buys, the best sells, what happened to last week's list, and five trades the calculator calls fair.

A buy-low is a player the dynasty draft market prices at least 35% under the expert consensus. A sell-high is the reverse, at least 35% over. Only players valued at 1,000 or more count, and today that is 15 buys and 35 sells. Values as of Nov 24, 2026. Fantasy Football Calculator had no dynasty data today, so the market side is Sleeper only. Change is measured against the final Nov 17 values on record, so a few differ slightly from last week's article. The always-current version lives on the buy-low and sell-high page.

James Cook III is the biggest fall in the table and the biggest among all 15 buys: down 1,409 in a week to 5,692, 45% under the experts. His last three average 18.5, close to his 18.7 for the year, so the price fell and the scoring did not. Ashton Jeanty is steady at 5,386, up 100, with the gap at 73% under the experts. Jonathan Taylor made the biggest jump of any buy, up 577 to 5,098, but his last three average 9.5 against 15.7 for the season.

Tyler Warren is the cautionary name. He is 60% under the experts, but his last three are 4.7 PPG and his price is down 630. A discount on a cold stretch is a hold, not a bargain. Breece Hall has the wide gap (77% under) on just 6 games played.

The quarterbacks need a format check. Josh Allen is 5,517 in 1QB and 9,143 in superflex. Brock Purdy is 2,790 in 1QB and 6,104 in superflex. If you play superflex, the price is a lot higher than the table shows.

A short Week 12 checklist

  1. Check the format. These are 1QB values. In superflex the quarterbacks jump: Josh Allen goes from 5,517 to 9,143.
  2. Read the status column before you pay for a discount.
  3. Look at the last-3 average. A buy with a falling average is a hold, not a bargain.
  4. Compare to Nov 17. A flag that survived a week of price moves is sturdier than a brand new one.
  5. Re-check before you offer. These are Nov 24, 2026 values, and values move every day. Open the league import tool to see which of these names sit on your roster.
