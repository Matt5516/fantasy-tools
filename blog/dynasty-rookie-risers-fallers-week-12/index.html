Dynasty Rookie Risers and Fallers, Week 12: Prices vs 2027 Picks

Week 12 dynasty rookie risers and fallers since Nov 17: KC Concepcion Jr. +699, Omar Cooper Jr. +409, Jonah Coleman -227, priced against 2027 picks.

As of Nov 24, 2026, KC Concepcion Jr. is the biggest rookie riser since Nov 17, +699 to 3,114 in 1QB leagues, and Omar Cooper Jr. is next at +409. Jonah Coleman is the biggest faller at -227 (3,012). Seven rookies are worth at least a 2027 Mid 1st (2,031).

By DynastyMath Staff · Published November 25, 2026 · Numbers as of November 24, 2026 · 7 min read

KC Concepcion Jr. gained 699 points in a week and is now the 4th most valuable rookie, up from 7th. Jordyn Tyson is third at 3,139. Jonah Coleman slipped from 3rd to 5th after a 227-point drop. Jeremiyah Love (5,550) and Carnell Tate (4,267) still hold the top two spots.

Every rookie below is priced against the 2027 pick that costs the same. Values are on a 0 to 10,000 scale as of Nov 24, 2026, and changes compare to Nov 17. Of the 87 first-year players with a value on both dates, 40 gained, 36 lost and 11 did not move. The tables skip rookies under 250 on both dates. Full class: dynasty rookie rankings.

KC Concepcion Jr. (CLE). Up 699 (+28.9%) to 3,114, the largest gain in the class by points. Experts have him at 3,015 and the Sleeper market at 3,296, so the price is not stretched. He has 10 games, 10 PPG and 12.4 over the last three.

Omar Cooper Jr. (NYJ). Up 409 to 2,825. Our data flags him as a sell: experts sit at 1,543 and Sleeper at 2,618. He has 6 games, 10.4 PPG and 19.5 over the last three, and he is listed Out.

Denzel Boston (CLE) and Makai Lemon (PHI). Boston added 362 to 2,894. Lemon added 308 to 1,770, and he is one of our buy flags: experts at 2,952, the Sleeper market at 878.

Ja'Kobi Lane (BAL). Lane has the biggest percent gain among rookies worth 250 or more, +72.2% (from 198 to 341), but he averages 3.9 PPG over 7 games.

Jonah Coleman (DEN). Down 227 (-7.0%) to 3,012, the biggest drop in the class by points. His production is loud: 6 games, 16.3 PPG and 24.7 over the last three. The market is louder than the experts, too. Sleeper has him at 3,101 and experts at 1,514, which makes him a sell flag even after the drop.

Emmett Johnson (KC). Down 212 (-26.6%) to 585, the largest percent drop among rookies worth 250 or more. He scores 22.9 a game over his last three against 12 for the season.

Jeremiyah Love (ARI). Down 164 (-2.9%) to 5,550. He is listed IR, and our data has no return date. Only 8 rookies worth 250 or more lost value this week, and the other five moved by 32 points or fewer.

What changed since the Week 11 board

Our values on Nov 18 give a clean comparison. Jonah Coleman is +52 against that day, Jordyn Tyson +228 and KC Concepcion Jr. +661. Jeremiyah Love is -164 and Carnell Tate -58. Emmett Johnson, who was 797 on Nov 18, is -212. A one-week jump is not a trend. Jordyn Tyson has played 3 games.

Superflex changes the QB price

Fernando Mendoza (LV) is worth 554 in 1QB leagues and 2,305 in superflex, the widest superflex premium in the class. In superflex that covers a 2027 Late 1st (1,100) and falls 49 short of a 2027 Mid 1st (2,354). He has 3 games, so the price rests on very little.

What to do with this

  1. Price the thin samples carefully. Jordyn Tyson has 3 games. A big move on a small sample can reverse.
  2. Check the tag. Jeremiyah Love is listed IR, Omar Cooper Jr. is listed Out and Denzel Boston is listed Questionable. Price that into any offer.
  3. Run the picks. Compare each rookie with the rookie pick values and load both sides into the trade analyzer.
  4. Rank before you offer. Build your list on the rookie draft board so you know your price before the other manager names theirs.
