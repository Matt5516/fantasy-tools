Rest-of-Season Rankings: Most Projected PPR Points, Weeks 9-11

Rest-of-season PPR rankings for Weeks 9-11 as of Nov 4, 2026: Jahmyr Gibbs leads at 76.0 projected points, CeeDee Lamb tops receivers at 60.0.

As of Nov 4, 2026, Jahmyr Gibbs has the most projected PPR points over Weeks 9-11 among players with all three weeks projected (76.0). CeeDee Lamb leads receivers at 60.0, Josh Allen leads quarterbacks at 74.7 and Brock Bowers leads tight ends at 52.2.

By DynastyMath Staff · Published November 4, 2026 · Numbers as of November 4, 2026 · 5 min read

Three weeks of projections tell you more than one. This is the board for Weeks 9, 10 and 11, with PPR projections added up and set beside what each player has scored. Numbers as of Nov 4, 2026.

How to read it. The 3-week total is the sum of the weekly projections and per week is that total divided by three. ROS score blends the average projection (40%), last-3 PPG (30%) and season PPG (30%). The position tables only include players with all three weeks projected. Players with two are in their own table further down, because their totals are not comparable.
