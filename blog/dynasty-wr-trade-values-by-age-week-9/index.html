Dynasty WR Trade Values by Age, Week 9

Dynasty WR trade values by age as of Nov 8, 2026: the top 10 receivers, three age bands, biggest movers since Oct 30 and four fair trades.

As of Nov 8, 2026, Jaxon Smith-Njigba (age 24, 10,184) is the top dynasty receiver. Receivers aged 27 to 29 cost the most per head at 6,725 points; Zay Flowers fell 2,267 since Oct 30 and Matthew Golden rose 1,041.

By DynastyMath Staff · Published November 8, 2026 · Numbers as of November 8, 2026 · 5 min read

Age sets the price at receiver, and the veterans still cost the most. Values as of Nov 8, 2026. Changes compare to Oct 30, the day of our Week 8 receiver piece.

Jaxon Smith-Njigba leads the board at 10,184, up 116 since Oct 30. He is 24 and scores 27.5 per game, the best PPG of the top 24 receivers. Puka Nacua (9,476) and Ja'Marr Chase (9,228) follow. Values use a 0-10,000 scale.

The 27-to-29 group is the priciest per head at 6,725. The 24-to-26 group is the biggest at 12 receivers and 72,762 points, and it is down 1,779 since Oct 30. The 23-and-under group is up 1,956, the best move of the three, led by Matthew Golden (+1,041) and Luther Burden III (+768). Changes are summed over today's members of each band.

The read: the market still pays a premium for 27-to-29 receivers, and money is moving toward the youngest tier. Rebuilders should sell a veteran into that gap. Buyers who can wait should get a 23-and-under receiver while the rise is early.

How to use this

  1. Find your receivers on the dynasty WR rankings and note each age band.
  2. Sell veterans in the 27-to-29 band after a price rise. Selling after a drop is selling low.
  3. Check a falling name like Zay Flowers or Chris Olave against his PPG before you move him.
  4. Build any swap in the trade analyzer and keep it inside 5%.
  5. Cross-check prices on the dynasty trade value chart. Drake London is the top-10 name with the biggest rise.

Frequently asked questions

What are dynasty WR trade values by age in Week 9?

As of Nov 8, 2026, the top-24 receivers aged 27 to 29 average 6,725 points, the 24-to-26 group 6,064 and the 23-and-under group 5,489. No receiver in the top 24 is 30 or older.

Who is the top dynasty receiver right now?

Jaxon Smith-Njigba, age 24, at 10,184 in 1QB and 9,249 in superflex. He scores 27.5 PPG. Puka Nacua is second at 9,476.

Which dynasty receivers rose the most since Oct 30?

Among receivers worth at least 2,500, Matthew Golden (+1,041 to 5,185), Jameson Williams (+973) and Omar Cooper Jr. (+840) rose the most. Zay Flowers fell the most, -2,267.

Is Chris Olave for Nico Collins a fair dynasty trade?

By our calculator, yes. 6,124 against 6,049 is a +1.2% gap, inside the 5% fair range.

Do superflex values change WR prices?

Yes. Every receiver in the top 10 is worth less in superflex than in 1QB. Smith-Njigba is 10,184 in 1QB and 9,249 in superflex. Check both in the trade analyzer.
