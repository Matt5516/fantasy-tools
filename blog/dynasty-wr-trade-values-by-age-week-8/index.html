Dynasty WR Trade Values by Age, Week 8

Dynasty WR trade values by age as of Oct 30, 2026: eight receivers 27+ against eight 24 and under, with weekly value changes, PPG and five fair trades.

As of Oct 30, 2026, the eight top-24 dynasty receivers aged 27+ are worth 50,178 points combined; the eight aged 24 and under are worth 44,828. Sell Tee Higgins (4,762, sell flag) and CeeDee Lamb after his +941 jump; buy Malik Nabers (6,924, age 23) and Emeka Egbuka (4,270, age 24).

By DynastyMath Staff · Published October 31, 2026 · Numbers as of October 30, 2026 · 6 min read

The age gap at receiver is closing, but the veterans still cost more. Values as of Oct 30, 2026.

Of the top 24 receivers by value, 8 are 27 or older and 8 are 24 or younger. The veterans are worth 50,178 points combined, down 1,212 since Oct 23. The young group is worth 44,828, up 1,114. That cuts the older group's lead from 7,676 to 5,350, so the market still pays full price for 27-year-olds. The best receiver on the board, Jaxon Smith-Njigba, is 24 and sits at 10,068.

Below: the veterans to sell, the young receivers to buy, and five trades that land within 5% on our calculator rule. Every value is on a 0-10,000 scale. "Flag" is our buy/sell signal: how far the draft market sits from the experts. FFC had no dynasty data today, so the draft market here is Sleeper only.

CeeDee Lamb is the best veteran and the loudest sell. At 9,448 he is the most valuable receiver in the 27+ group, and he is up 941 (11.1%) since Oct 23, the biggest rise of the eight. He scores 22.2 per game but just 14.4 over his last three, the widest drop from season pace to recent pace in this group. A rising price on a falling box score is the setup you sell into.

Christian Watson is the opposite. He is down 1,425 (22.5%) to 4,904, the largest fall of all 16 receivers in this piece, and he is listed Questionable. He still scores 18.4 per game with 12.5 over his last three. Nico Collins is the next biggest faller of the veterans, down 686 (9.7%) to 6,391, with 8 per game over his last three against 15.2 for the season. He has played 5 games.

Tee Higgins is the only veteran carrying a flag: sell, with the market 70% above the experts. His value is up 568 (13.5%) at 4,762. Justin Jefferson is up 704 (9.6%) to 8,016 after 5 games, scoring 14.4 per game and 10.8 over his last three. Amon-Ra St. Brown is down 635 (6.9%) at 8,506, yet his 21.4 per game is the second best in the group.

DeVonta Smith and A.J. Brown are both listed Questionable, and so is Watson. Smith has played 4 games and Brown 3, so their per-game numbers rest on small samples. Brown has the lowest value (3,508) and the lowest PPG (6.4) of the eight.

Jaxon Smith-Njigba is the price of admission at 10,068, 28 per game, the highest PPG of all 16 receivers. His value barely moved (-51). Parker Washington (age 24) is up 240 (5.0%) at 5,003 and carries a sell flag, the market 38% above the experts. He works as a swap piece in the trades below, but he is not a bargain.

Tetairoa McMillan is the only young receiver down more than 100, off 436 (6.9%) to 5,892. He scores 11.5 over his last three against 15.6 on the season.

Emeka Egbuka carries a buy flag, the market 54% below the experts, but he is listed Questionable. Carnell Tate, the youngest of the 16 at 21, is also Questionable and up 233 (5.9%). Luther Burden III is flat (+22) and Matthew Golden is up 256 (6.6%) to 4,144.

  1. List every receiver on your roster who is 27 or older and look up his value on his player page.
  2. A sell flag or a price that rose this week is the best time to move a veteran. A price that fell means you are selling low.
  3. Run the swap in the trade analyzer before you message anyone.
  4. Contenders: keep the veteran if he starts for you. Rebuilders: sell now.

Frequently asked questions

At what age do dynasty wide receivers lose value?

As of Oct 30, 2026, 8 of the top 24 receivers are 27 or older, and the group is worth 50,178 points against 44,828 for the 24-and-under group. Veterans still hold full price, which is why this is a sell window.

Which wide receivers should I sell in dynasty right now?

CeeDee Lamb (9,448, up 941 this week), Justin Jefferson (8,016) and Tee Higgins (4,762) have all risen since Oct 23. Higgins carries our sell flag. Amon-Ra St. Brown (8,506), Nico Collins (6,391) and Christian Watson (4,904) have fallen, so selling them now means selling low.

Which young wide receivers are dynasty buys?

Malik Nabers (age 23, 6,924) and Emeka Egbuka (age 24, 4,270) carry our buy flag, meaning the draft market is at least 35% below the experts. Egbuka is listed Questionable. Parker Washington carries a sell flag.

Is Christian Watson for Parker Washington a fair dynasty trade?

By our calculator, yes. 4,904 against 5,003 is a -2.0% gap, inside the 5% fair range. Watson is listed Questionable, so check his status first.

Do these values change for superflex?

Yes, each receiver's superflex value is lower than his 1QB value. Our tables show both. Price your own deal in the trade analyzer.
