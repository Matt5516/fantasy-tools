Dynasty Rookie Risers and Fallers, Week 11: Prices vs 2027 Picks

Week 11 dynasty rookie risers and fallers since Nov 11: Jordyn Tyson +1,297, Jonah Coleman +1,052, Germie Bernard -815, priced against 2027 picks.

As of Nov 18, 2026, Jordyn Tyson is the biggest rookie riser since Nov 11, +1,297 to 2,911 in 1QB leagues, and Jonah Coleman is up 1,052 to 2,960. Germie Bernard is the biggest faller at -815 (638). Seven rookies are now worth at least a 2027 Mid 1st (2,024).

By DynastyMath Staff · Published November 18, 2026 · Numbers as of November 18, 2026 · 6 min read

Jonah Coleman climbed from sixth in the class to third. Jordyn Tyson jumped from seventh to fourth after sitting at 1,614 a week ago. Germie Bernard went the other way, from eighth to fifteenth. At the top, Jeremiyah Love (5,714) and Carnell Tate (4,325) still hold the first two spots.

This is the Week 11 rookie board, with a twist: every rookie is priced against the 2027 pick that costs the same. Values are on a 0 to 10,000 scale as of Nov 18, 2026, and changes compare to Nov 11. 93 first-year players carry a value. Of the 87 that had one on both dates, 46 gained, 28 lost and 13 did not move. The tables skip rookies under 250 on both dates. Full class: dynasty rookie rankings.

Jordyn Tyson (NO). Up 1,297 (+80.4%) to 2,911, the largest gain in the class by points. He has played 2 games and averages 12 PPR points, so the sample is thin. Experts have him at 3,079 and the Sleeper market at 2,687, which is close.

Jonah Coleman (DEN). Up 1,052 (+55.1%) to 2,960, and he now sits between a 2027 Mid 1st and a 2027 Early 1st. Be careful: Sleeper has him at 5,324 and experts at 1,324, so our data flags him as a sell on the market gap. He has 5 games, 12.6 PPG and 16 over the last three.

Emmett Johnson (KC). The biggest percent gain among rookies worth 250 or more, +196.3% (from 269 to 797), moving from 17th to 11th in the class. He has 9 games, 10.7 PPG and 23.3 over the last three.

Omar Cooper Jr. (NYJ) and KC Concepcion Jr. (CLE). Omar Cooper Jr. is up 374 to 2,378 and KC Concepcion Jr. is up 272 to 2,453. KC Concepcion Jr. still carries a buy flag, with experts at 3,022 and Sleeper at 1,576.

Germie Bernard (PIT). Down 815 (-56.1%) to 638, the biggest drop in the class by points and by percent. A week ago he was a riser at 1,453. He has 9 games, 7.5 PPG and 4.9 over the last three. Experts have him at 727 and Sleeper at 359.

The other three fallers moved by 32 points or fewer. Only 4 rookies worth 250 or more lost value, so this was a week for gains: nothing else in the class dropped like Germie Bernard.

In superflex that covers a 2027 Late 1st (1,099) and falls short of a 2027 Mid 1st (2,342). He has 2 games, so the price rests on very little. Experts have him at 797 and the Sleeper market at 256.

What to do with this

  1. Price the one-sample rookies carefully. Jordyn Tyson has played 2 games. A big jump on a small sample can reverse, as it did for Germie Bernard.
  2. Check the tag. Jeremiyah Love is listed IR and Carnell Tate is listed Questionable. Price that into any offer. Our data has no return dates.
  3. Price a pick against a rookie. Use the rookie pick values and run both sides through the trade analyzer.
  4. Rank before you offer. Build your list on the rookie draft board so you know your price before the other manager names theirs.
