Dynasty Value Risers and Fallers Week 11: Buy, Sell or Hold

The biggest Week 11 dynasty value risers and fallers, led by Javonte Williams (+1,918) and Kyren Williams (-1,847), with a buy, sell or hold call on each.

The biggest dynasty value riser since Nov 9 is Javonte Williams at +1,918 (+53.9%), now 5,477. The biggest faller is Kyren Williams at -1,847 (-34.3%), now 3,538. 6 of the 10 risers carry a sell flag, and 3 of the 10 fallers are buys, led by Jonathan Taylor. As of Nov 16, 2026.

By DynastyMath Staff · Published November 16, 2026 · Numbers as of November 16, 2026 · 7 min read

Dynasty prices move every day. A week is long enough to show who is really climbing and who is sliding. Here are the 10 biggest risers and the 10 biggest fallers going into Week 11, each with a buy, sell or hold call. Values as of Nov 16, 2026, on our 0-10,000 scale, 1QB. Change is today's value minus the value on Nov 9, which is also the date of our Week 10 risers and fallers.

The rule: players valued at 1,000 or more on either date, ranked by raw points gained or lost. We can't see why a value moved, so we don't guess. We only report where it went. The draft market below is Sleeper only: FFC had no dynasty data today.

These 10 added 11,242 points between them. Javonte Williams leads at +1,918 (+53.9%), now 5,477 and RB7. Last week he was one of our fallers. His last three games average 12.3 PPG, under his season 16.7, so the price is running ahead of the scoring. The draft market has him at 9,249 against 2,706 from the experts, and that is a sell flag. When the price runs this far past the experts, you sell into it.

6 of the 10 carry a sell flag, and none carry a buy flag. That means the draft market sits at least 35% above the experts. Isaiah Likely is up 1,207 (41.6%) to 4,111, with the market at 6,261 against 1,773. Tee Higgins is up 1,180 (23.3%) to 6,244: market 8,559, experts 3,787. Dak Prescott is up 746 (51.9%) to 2,183 at age 33, and C.J. Stroud is up 721 (35.1%) to 2,773. In superflex those two are worth 5,516 and 6,426, which is why quarterback sells depend on your format.

The rookies. Jordyn Tyson has the largest percentage gain of the group, +57.6%, to 3,055, and he has played only 2 games. The experts (3,079) and Sleeper (3,022) agree, so there is no flag. That is a hold on a small sample. Jonah Coleman is the other rookie, up 1,043 (54.4%) to 2,960, with a market (5,324) well above the experts (1,324).

These 10 lost 10,436 points. Kyren Williams fell furthest, -1,847 (-34.3%), to 3,538. He has no flag, and the market (3,604) sits right next to the experts (3,463). His season average is 16.4 PPG and his last three is 9.1. With no gap between experts and market, that is a hold, not a bargain.

3 of the 10 fallers are buys. Our rule gives a buy to a player who is not listed Out or on IR and either carries a buy flag or is a faller averaging 15 or more PPG over his last three. Jonathan Taylor is down 1,279 (21.7%) to 4,626 and RB10 at age 27. The market has him at 2,890 and the experts at 6,578, a gap of 56% below. Brock Purdy is down 1,231 (29.7%) to 2,912, with a market of 1,391 against 3,008. He averages 23.1 PPG, and in superflex he is worth 6,370. Trevor Lawrence is the buy without a flag: down 670 (17.0%) to 3,279, with 19.9 PPG over his last three and 6,437 in superflex.

2 fallers carry a sell flag, even after the drop. Chuba Hubbard is down 1,001 (24.7%) to 3,049, yet the market (3,463) is still far above the experts (1,140). Jaylen Warren is down 982 (32.1%) to 2,079, with the market at 2,706 and the experts at 1,543.

The holds. Matthew Stafford is 38, the oldest faller, and lost 1,172 (69.8%), to 508. That is the largest percentage drop of the group. In superflex he is worth 1,989. Kenneth Walker III and Mike Evans are listed Out. Our data gives a status tag and nothing about a return date, so both are holds.

Buy, sell or hold: the count

Across the 20 players in the two tables that comes to 8 sells, 3 buys and 9 holds. Sell and buy follow the rules above; anyone listed Out or on IR is a hold.

How to use this

  1. Sell the flagged risers first. If you own Javonte Williams, Isaiah Likely or Tee Higgins, price them now at the trade analyzer.
  2. Buy the healthy fallers with a flag. Jonathan Taylor and Brock Purdy dropped and carry buy flags. Trevor Lawrence dropped with no flag but averages 19.9 PPG over his last three.
  3. Check status before you pay. Listed Out or IR means the price can move again.
  4. Mind the format. These values are 1QB. In superflex, C.J. Stroud is 6,426 and Brock Purdy is 6,370.
  5. Keep watching. The live risers and fallers page updates every day. This list does not.

Frequently asked questions

Who are the biggest dynasty value risers in Week 11?

As of Nov 16, 2026, since Nov 9: Javonte Williams at +1,918 (+53.9%), James Cook III at +1,495 (+26.7%), Isaiah Likely at +1,207 (+41.6%) and Tee Higgins at +1,180 (+23.3%).

Who are the biggest dynasty value fallers in Week 11?

Kyren Williams at -1,847 (-34.3%), Jonathan Taylor at -1,279 (-21.7%) and Brock Purdy at -1,231 (-29.7%) fell the most in points.

Should I sell a player whose value just jumped?

If the draft market is already at least 35% above the experts, yes. 6 of the 10 risers meet that test, including Javonte Williams and Isaiah Likely.

Is a value drop a buy-low signal?

Only when the player is not listed Out or on IR and the market sits well below the experts. Jonathan Taylor and Brock Purdy fit. Trevor Lawrence has no flag but averages 19.9 PPG over his last three, so he is a buy too. Kyren Williams has no flag and 9.1 over his last three, so he is a hold.
