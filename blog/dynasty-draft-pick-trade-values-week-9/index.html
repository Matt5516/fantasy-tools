Dynasty Draft Pick Trade Values Week 9: What Each Pick Buys

Week 9 dynasty draft pick trade values: a 2027 early 1st is 4,386 in 1QB and 5,220 in superflex. See the healthy starters each pick buys and 12 fair deals.

As of Nov 6, 2026, a 2027 early 1st is 4,386 in 1QB and 5,220 in superflex, with 35 players priced above it in 1QB and 23 in superflex. A mid 1st is 1,985 (2,317), a late 1st 928 (1,086) and a 2028 1st 1,605 (1,888). Superflex adds 19% to the early 1st and 17% to the mid 1st.

By DynastyMath Staff · Published November 6, 2026 · Numbers as of November 6, 2026 · 5 min read

Picks only matter if you know what they turn into. Here is the price of each pick, the healthy starter at every position it lands next to, and the deals the calculator calls fair. Values as of Nov 6, 2026, week 9 of the regular season. Fantasy Football Calculator had no dynasty data that day, so the draft-market side comes from Sleeper dynasty drafts.

Picks and players share one 0 to 10,000 scale, so you can trade one for the other without a conversion table. Superflex has its own set of numbers.

An early 2027 1st is 9.6 early 2nds in 1QB. A mid 1st is 4.3. A late 1st is 2.0. If someone offers you two seconds for a first, check which first.

The early 1st sits behind 35 players in 1QB and 23 in superflex. That is the price of a top-40 asset in 1QB and a top-25 asset in superflex, not a lottery ticket.

Superflex: who pays more

Every pick costs more in superflex. The mid 1st gets the smallest bump of the four firsts, +17%. The early 1st gets the biggest, +19%. The late 2nd climbs +19%, the only other pick that rounds to the same top bump.

Quarterbacks show the gap. Bryce Young is 1,299 in 1QB and 3,518 in superflex. Jordan Love is 1,698 and 4,876. In superflex Jordan Love sits 6.6% below the early 1st (5,220) and Bryce Young sits 33% below it. In 1QB both cost less than half of that pick. Same player, two very different asks.

The quarterback angle. In superflex, Dak Prescott (33) is 5,460, 4.6% above the early 1st at 5,220. In 1QB the closest quarterback to that pick is Joe Burrow (29) at 4,701, a 7.2% miss, so a straight swap is not fair there.

How to use this

  1. Name the slot and the year. Early, mid, late and 2028 are four different prices.
  2. Quote your format. The superflex number is the one to use in a superflex league.
  3. Pay for the second piece at a discount. Two picks are worth less than their sum.
  4. Shop the position you need. Use the position table to see which pick covers a QB, RB, WR or TE.
  5. Check the exact pick. Run it through the dynasty trade analyzer, browse the rookie pick values and see the class on the rookie draft board.

Frequently asked questions

What is a 2027 1st round pick worth in dynasty?

As of Nov 6, 2026, a 2027 early 1st is 4,386 in 1QB and 5,220 in superflex. A mid 1st is 1,985 and 2,317. A late 1st is 928 and 1,086.

How much more is a pick worth in superflex?

Every pick is higher. The four firsts range from +17% (mid 2027 1st) to +19% (early 2027 1st). The late 2027 2nd is +19%.

How many 2nd round picks equal a 1st?

Not as many as it sounds, because of the depth discount. In raw value an early 1st is 9.6 early 2nds, a mid 1st 4.3 and a late 1st 2.0. Use the trade analyzer for the discounted total.

Is a 2028 1st worth more than a late 2027 1st?

Yes. A 2028 1st is 1,605 in 1QB and 1,888 in superflex, against 928 and 1,086 for a 2027 late 1st. It is below a mid 2027 1st (1,985 and 2,317).

Where do these pick prices come from?

They use the same expert and draft-market values that price players. Sleeper dynasty drafts supplied market data as of Nov 6, 2026; Fantasy Football Calculator had no dynasty data that day.
