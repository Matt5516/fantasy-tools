Superflex QB Trade Values Week 12: Who Rose and Who Fell

Superflex QB trade values for Week 12 as of Nov 26, 2026: J.J. McCarthy +1,657, Bo Nix -1,507 since Nov 20, plus 5 fair trades.

As of Nov 26, 2026, Joe Burrow is still the top superflex asset at 9,398 (5,872 in 1QB), 865 ahead of Josh Allen. Since Nov 20, J.J. McCarthy rose the most among QBs (+1,657 to 2,708) and Bo Nix fell the most (-1,507 to 3,462). 6 of the top 12 superflex assets are QBs. In 1QB, none are.

By DynastyMath Staff · Published November 27, 2026 · Numbers as of November 26, 2026 · 6 min read

Last week's biggest riser is this week's biggest faller. Bo Nix jumped 1,584 between Nov 13 and Nov 20, and he has given back 1,507. Joe Burrow held the top spot. Patrick Mahomes dropped hard. If you priced a quarterback a week ago, that price is stale. Values use a 0 to 10,000 scale, as of Nov 26, 2026. Changes compare to Nov 20, the date of last week's list. 1QB value is the price in a one-QB league. Superflex value is the price when a second QB can start. FFC had no dynasty data today, so the draft-market side comes from Sleeper.

J.J. McCarthy is the biggest riser at +1,657 (+157.7%), from 1,051 to 2,708, which moves him from 107th to 61st. Geno Smith added 1,500 to reach 2,382. Tyler Shough gained 1,418, most of the 1,559 he lost the week before. Jared Goff gained 1,328 and is at 4,651.

Matthew Stafford fell 1,178. Look at the 1QB column before you panic. The same player can move far less in a one-QB league.

Our data does not say why. A riser can be a sell. A faller can be a buy. The price is the point. If you held J.J. McCarthy on Nov 20, you held 1,051 of superflex value. Today it is 2,708.

How many QBs are premium assets?

In 1QB, 0 of the top 12 players by value are quarterbacks, and the first one on the list is Joe Burrow at No. 19. In superflex, 6 of the top 12 are QBs, and 13 of the top 24. A QB ranked deep in 1QB can still cost real value once the second slot opens. Joe Burrow has the smallest superflex premium of the top 24 QBs (1.6x), because his 1QB value is already high. How to use this

  1. Quote the right column. Use the superflex number in superflex leagues and the 1QB number in 1QB leagues.
  2. Check the change since Nov 20. A QB up 1,657 is priced differently than he was a week ago. Counter with today's number.
  3. Check the tag. Status is a label in our data, not a timeline.
  4. Run the deal. The trade analyzer uses the same rule as the table above.
