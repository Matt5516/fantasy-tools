Week 7 Fantasy Breakouts: Last-3 PPG Beats Season PPG

Week 7 fantasy breakouts as of Oct 24, 2026: ten players whose last-3 PPR PPG tops season PPG, with Week 7 projections, next-3 sums and cooling-off names.

As of Oct 24, 2026, Malik Nabers has the biggest jump in Week 7: 26.9 PPR points per game over his last three against 17.1 for the season (+9.8). Drake Maye is the only quarterback in the top ten and carries the top Week 7 projection of the group (21.7).

By DynastyMath Staff · Published October 25, 2026 · Numbers as of October 24, 2026 · 5 min read

Three games is a trend, not a verdict. It is still the cleanest signal you get in Week 7. Here are the players whose last-3 PPR average runs ahead of their season average, plus the ones sliding the other way. Numbers as of Oct 24, 2026.

The pool: 150 players with at least 4 games played, a full 3-game last-3 window and a season average of 8.0 PPR points or better. The floor cuts low-scoring noise, where one 12-point game moves everything. Of those 150, 81 are running above their season pace and 69 below. The ten biggest gaps are below, ranked by last-3 minus season.

Malik Nabers leads at +9.8, from 17.1 to 26.9. He has played 6 games, so half of that sample is the hot stretch. Treat it as heat, not a new baseline. Drake Maye is the only quarterback in the ten. He averaged 20.9 for the season and has pushed to 30.2.

The rest splits into 2 running backs, 4 receivers and 3 tight ends. Kyle Pitts Sr. (ATL) moved from 8.3 to 15.3. Colston Loveland (CHI) moved from 8.9 to 14.1. Isaiah Likely (NYG) has the best last-3 average of any tight end in the pool at 23.3, and he was already at 18.2 for the season.

None of the ten carries an injury tag in our data. Tyquan Thornton would rank 16th on this list at +3.8, but he is listed IR, so leave him out of lineup math.

How to use this

  1. Start with this week's projection. Last-3 is the tiebreaker, not the verdict.
  2. Open the start/sit tool and compare two similar players side by side.
  3. Check the Weeks column before you trust a next-3 sum. A two-week sum is not comparable with a three-week one.
  4. For the longer view, the rest-of-season rankings blend this week's projection, the last 3 games and the season average.
  5. Skip any name with an injury tag until his status is clear.

Frequently asked questions

Which fantasy players are breaking out in Week 7?

As of Oct 24, 2026, the biggest gaps between last-3 and season PPR average are Malik Nabers (+9.8), Drake Maye (+9.3), Kyle Pitts Sr. (+7.0) and DeMario Douglas (+6.6). The pool is players with at least 4 games and a season average of 8.0 or better.

Is last-3 PPG better than season PPG for start/sit?

Neither alone. Last-3 is a small sample, and the Week 7 projection for all ten risers sits below their last-3 average. Use last-3 to break ties between players with similar projections.

Who is cooling off in fantasy football this week?

The biggest drops from season average to last-3 average are Amon-Ra St. Brown (-7.4), Jaxon Smith-Njigba (-7.2), Caleb Williams (-5.8). All three have a Week 7 projection above their last-3 average.

Should I add a player just because his last 3 games are hot?

Check the projection first. Drake Maye is projected for 21.7 this week, the top number among the ten risers, but every one of the ten is projected below his last-3 pace. Read the Weeks column too: some next-3 sums cover only two weeks.
