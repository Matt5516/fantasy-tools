Week 11 Fantasy Injury Report: Projections for Banged-Up Players

Week 11 fantasy injury report as of Nov 18, 2026: PPR projections for 25 of 32 Questionable players, IR names and healthy fill-ins.

As of Nov 18, 2026, 32 players are listed Questionable, 1 Doubtful, 23 Out, 87 on IR, 3 PUP and 1 DNR. Kenneth Walker III has the top Questionable Week 11 projection at 18.3 PPR, ahead of Geno Smith (15.8) and Brock Bowers (14.8).

By DynastyMath Staff · Published November 19, 2026 · Numbers as of November 18, 2026 · 4 min read

A tag tells you who is listed. The projection tells you what the player is worth in your lineup. Here is every listed status for Week 11, what each group projects, and who you can slot in next to them. All points are PPR, as of Nov 18, 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Which Questionable players have the highest Week 11 projections?

As of Nov 18, 2026: Kenneth Walker III (18.3), Geno Smith (15.8), Brock Bowers (14.8), Mike Evans (13.2), Carnell Tate (13), all in PPR points.

Who is a healthy fill-in for Kenneth Walker III?
