Week 8 Start/Sit by Projection Tiers: QB, RB, WR and TE

Week 8 start/sit tiers for QB, RB, WR and TE on PPR projections, with last-3 PPG, 7 Out players still projected and flex ties at 17.2 and 16.9.

As of Oct 27, 2026, the top Week 8 PPR projections are Jaxon Smith-Njigba (25.1), Josh Allen (24.4) and Jahmyr Gibbs (23.1), with Trey McBride (19.3) the top tight end. Lamar Jackson still shows a 20.1 projection but is listed Out. Two flex pairs tie, Amon-Ra St. Brown and Drake London at 17.2 and Brock Bowers and Chuba Hubbard at 16.9.

By DynastyMath Staff · Published October 27, 2026 · Numbers as of October 27, 2026 · 6 min read

Start the high projection. Break ties with recent scoring. Every number is PPR points for Week 8, as of Oct 27, 2026. One trap first: 7 players listed Out still carry a projection of 10 or more, Lamar Jackson's 20.1 among them. They are left out of the tiers below.

How the tiers work. Within each position, Tier 1 is any player less than 3 points below the top projection. Tier 2 is 3 to 6 points back, and Tier 3 is 6 to 9. Players in a tier are close enough to swap. Pick Tier 1 before you think about anything else.

Frequently asked questions

Who are the top fantasy quarterbacks to start in Week 8?

By PPR projection as of Oct 27, 2026: Josh Allen (24.4), Dak Prescott (22.9), Drake Maye (22.3), Joe Burrow (21.4). Lamar Jackson shows 20.1 but is listed Out.

Who should I start at flex in Week 8?

Two pairs tie. Amon-Ra St. Brown and Drake London are both 17.2 and both Questionable. Brock Bowers and Chuba Hubbard are both 16.9. Last-three PPG separates them: 17.5 to 16.9 in the first pair and 18.3 to 15.8 in the second.

Is Jahmyr Gibbs a must-start in Week 8?

Yes by the numbers. He is projected for 23.1, the highest running back projection, and averages 28.5 per game over 6 games.

Does a projection mean a player is playing?

No. 7 players listed Out still carry a Week 8 projection of 10 or more, led by Lamar Jackson at 20.1. Check the injury tag first.

Should I trust projections or recent scoring?

Use projections first and recent scoring as the tie-breaker. When the last-three is more than a few points away from the projection, treat it as a flag, not an answer.
