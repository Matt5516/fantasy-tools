Dynasty Buy-Low and Sell-High Trade Targets Week 9

Week 9 dynasty buy-low and sell-high trade targets as of Nov 4, 2026: 22 buy flags, 33 sell flags, 6 Week 8 names that lost their flag, six fair trades.

As of Nov 4, 2026, the top dynasty buy-low targets are Ashton Jeanty (5,857) and James Cook III (5,597), priced 76% and 50% under the experts. The top sell-high names are Christian McCaffrey (5,792) and Kyren Williams (5,597), with the draft market 55% and 99% above them. 6 of the 16 names on the Week 8 list lost their flag.

By DynastyMath Staff · Published November 4, 2026 · Numbers as of November 4, 2026 · 5 min read

A week is long in dynasty. The Week 8 list had 16 names. 10 of them still carry a flag today and 6 do not. This Week 9 board shows who dropped off, who joined, and which trades the calculator calls fair.

A buy-low is a player the dynasty draft market prices at least 35% under the expert consensus. A sell-high is the reverse, at least 35% over. Only players valued at 1,000 or more count, and today that is 22 buys and 33 sells. Values as of Nov 4, 2026. Fantasy Football Calculator had no dynasty data that day, so the market side is Sleeper only. The always-current version lives on the buy-low and sell-high page.

A short Week 9 checklist

  1. Check the format. These are 1QB values. In superflex the quarterbacks jump, as Caleb Williams shows.
  2. Read the status column before you pay for a discount.
  3. Look at the last-3 average. A buy with a falling average is a hold, not a bargain.
  4. Price the other side. The trade value chart shows what a fair return looks like for your sell.
  5. Re-check before you offer. These are Nov 4, 2026 values, and values move every day.
