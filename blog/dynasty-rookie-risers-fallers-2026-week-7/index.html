2026 Dynasty Rookie Risers and Fallers After Week 6

See which 2026 dynasty rookies gained and lost the most trade value since Oct 14, plus their games, PPG, injuries and what a 2027 first costs.

As of Oct 20, 2026, Germie Bernard is the biggest 2026 dynasty rookie riser, up 863 points (182.1%) since Oct 14 to 1,337, and Jadarian Price is the biggest faller among rookies valued at 1,000 or more, down 313 points (18.0%) to 1,425. The top rookie by value is still Carnell Tate at 4,354, ahead of Jeremiyah Love (4,147).

By DynastyMath Staff · Published October 20, 2026 · Numbers as of October 20, 2026 · 4 min read

Six weeks in, the 2026 rookie class has split into a handful of real assets and a long tail. 84 first-year players carry a dynasty value today. Since Oct 14, 49 have gained value and 21 have lost it. Here are the top ten, the biggest risers and fallers, and what 2027 and 2028 rookie picks cost against them. Values as of Oct 20, 2026, on a 0 to 10,000 scale. The risers

Germie Bernard (PIT, WR40). Up 863 points since Oct 14, from 474 to 1,337. He has 8.9 PPR points per game and 14.4 over his last three, and the sources are far apart: experts at 158, the draft market at 2,060 and production at 3,036. That makes him a possible sell: the market is well above the experts. A 182.1% jump in six days shows how fast a rookie's number can move.

Omar Cooper (NYJ). Up 497 (84.7%) to 1,084. He has played only 2 games, scoring 13.2 PPR points per game, so treat the production as a very small sample.

KC Concepcion (CLE). Up 363 (30.6%) to 1,550, with 6.6 PPG over 6 games and a Week 7 projection of 9.8. Teammate Denzel Boston (CLE) moved less, +122 (+5.6%) to 2,287, but has the better production at 11.9 PPG.

The fallers

Among rookies valued at 1,000 or more on either date, the biggest drops are:

  • Jadarian Price: -313 (-18.0%), now 1,425, status IR
  • Makai Lemon: -167 (-10.8%), now 1,379, status Questionable
  • Jordyn Tyson: -157 (-4.6%), now 3,258, status IR

Two of the three are injury cases: Jadarian Price is listed IR and Jordyn Tyson is listed IR. The status is listed. The timeline isn't, so don't assume the injury is why a value moved. Makai Lemon (PHI) is a different case: healthy apart from a questionable tag, scoring 5 PPG, with the experts at 2,687 and the market at 265. That gap makes him a possible buy, but a rookie scoring 5 points a game has a lot to prove.

Three ways to use this before your deadline

  1. Sell a small sample. If a rookie has played one or two games and shows a high points-per-game figure, as Omar Cooper does with 2 games, the number can fall as quickly as it rose.
  2. Be careful with one-source values. A rookie valued only by the expert consensus, like Jordyn Tyson, has no market or production signal to confirm the price.
  3. Price picks against players. If the trade analyzer says a 2027 mid 1st buys a current rookie plus a bench piece, decide whether you prefer a known role over a draft slot.
