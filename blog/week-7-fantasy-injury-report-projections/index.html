Week 7 Fantasy Injury Report: Projections for Questionable Starters

Week 7 fantasy injury report: projected PPR points for 10 Questionable starters, players with no projection, and same-position players projected close.

As of Oct 22, 2026, 52 players are listed Questionable and 11 Out. The top Questionable projection is Lamar Jackson at 20.9 PPR, ahead of Jordan Love (19.4) and Christian Watson (17.8); 87 players listed Out or IR carry no Week 7 projection.

By DynastyMath Staff · Published October 23, 2026 · Numbers as of October 22, 2026 · 5 min read

A Questionable tag is not a zero. Check the projection before you panic. Here is what the Week 7 numbers say about the biggest names on the list. Everything is PPR points, as of Oct 22, 2026.

As of that date, 52 players are listed Questionable and 11 are listed Out. We only use the status field and the projection, season average and last-3 average. No timelines, no news, no guesses about who plays. For live status, check the full injury report before kickoff.

Frequently asked questions

Which Questionable players have the highest Week 7 projections?

As of Oct 22, 2026: Lamar Jackson (20.9), Jordan Love (19.4), Christian Watson (17.8), Justin Jefferson (16.9) and Bucky Irving (16.9), all in PPR points.

Does a Questionable tag mean a player is out?

No. The tag is a status label, not a projection. In our data, 17 Questionable players carry a Week 7 projection of 10 or more points. Check the injury report before lineups lock.

Who has a similar projection to Lamar Jackson?

Brock Purdy (20.5) and Drake Maye (21.6) are within 1.0 point of his 20.9, and neither has an injury tag in our data.

Why does an Out or IR player show no projection?

Many do not. 87 players listed Out or IR have no Week 7 projection in our data, so there is nothing to compare. Jordan Mason is an exception: listed IR, projected 9.6. Do not lean on a projection for a player listed Out or IR.

Where do I check the latest status?

Use the injury report and the start/sit tool. Status changes after this data date, so verify before kickoff.
