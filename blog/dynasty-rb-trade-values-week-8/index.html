Dynasty RB Trade Values Week 8: Tiers, Movers and Fair Swaps

Dynasty RB trade values for Week 8 as of Nov 1, 2026: top 12 backs in three tiers, 2,078-point Love drop, and four fair swaps within 5%.

As of Nov 1, 2026, Jahmyr Gibbs (9,455), Bijan Robinson (9,226) and Kenneth Walker III (8,281) lead dynasty running backs on 1QB value. Among the top 24 RBs, Chase Brown rose the most since Oct 25 (+1,360) and Jeremiyah Love fell the most (-2,078).

This is the running back market in one place: the top 12 by 1QB value, how they moved since Oct 25, and four trades that land within 5%. Values run on a 0-10,000 scale. "Flag" is the gap between experts and the draft market. A buy flag means the market sits at least 35% below the experts. A sell flag means at least 35% above. Tier one: the three you pay full price for

Jahmyr Gibbs (9,455), Bijan Robinson (9,226) and Kenneth Walker III (8,281) are the only backs above 8,000. The biggest gap in the top 12 comes right after them: 1,674 points from Kenneth Walker III down to James Cook III. Gibbs scores 28.5 per game and 24.2 over his last three, the best of the 12 on both counts. His value is +44 since Oct 25.

Kenneth Walker III is the only one of the three up more than 100 (+135). Bijan Robinson is flat (+5), and his box score has slipped: 20.8 per game on the season, 13.4 over his last three. That is the widest drop from season pace to recent pace among the 12. His value has barely moved.

Tier two: five thousand to eight thousand

Six backs live here, and the market is split on them. James Cook III (6,607, age 27) is down 303 with no flag. Ashton Jeanty and Jonathan Taylor carry buy flags. Jonathan Taylor fell 763 to 6,083, the biggest drop in the tier, and still scores 19.6 per game. Ashton Jeanty (age 22) is down 194 to 6,265.

Christian McCaffrey, Kyren Williams and Javonte Williams carry sell flags. Christian McCaffrey is the oldest of the 12 at 30, listed Questionable, and up 160 to 5,769. A sell flag plus a rising price is a window. Kyren Williams is down 248 at 5,600. Javonte Williams is up 52 to 5,151.

Tier three: under five thousand

Chase Brown is the riser of the group: +1,360 (+38.3%) to 4,913, the biggest jump of any top-24 back and the only top-12 back up more than 1,000. He also carries a sell flag, and his 12.5 per game is the second-lowest of the 12. A price that jumps while the scoring does not is a sell-high setup.

Jeremiyah Love is the opposite. At age 21, the youngest of the 12, he fell 2,078 (-33.8%) to 4,068, the biggest drop of any top-24 back and the only top-12 back down more than 1,000. He carries a buy flag. He has played 5 games at 10.2 per game. Breece Hall also carries a buy flag, but he is Questionable and has played only 3 games, the fewest of the 12. Treat his 14.2 per game as a small sample.
