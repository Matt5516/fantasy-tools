Superflex QB Trade Values Week 8: Who Rose and Who Fell

Superflex QB trade values for Week 8 as of Oct 27, 2026: Dak Prescott +2,082, Bo Nix -2,038 since Oct 22, plus 10 fair trades.

As of Oct 27, 2026, Josh Allen is still the top superflex asset at 10,232 (7,009 in 1QB). Since Oct 22, Dak Prescott gained the most in superflex value (+2,082 to 6,138) and Bo Nix lost the most (-2,038 to 3,278). 5 of the top 12 superflex assets are quarterbacks; in 1QB, 1 is.

By DynastyMath Staff · Published October 28, 2026 · Numbers as of October 27, 2026 · 6 min read

Five days moved the quarterback market. Some prices jumped, some dropped, and last week's fair trades no longer match. Here is the Week 8 reset. Values use a 0 to 10,000 scale, as of Oct 27, 2026. How to use this

  1. Quote the right column. Use the superflex number in superflex leagues and the 1QB number in 1QB leagues.
  2. Check the five-day change. A QB up 2,082 is priced differently than he was on Oct 22. Counter with today's number.
  3. Check the tag. Status is a label in our data, not a timeline.
  4. Run the deal. The trade analyzer uses the same rule as the tables above.
