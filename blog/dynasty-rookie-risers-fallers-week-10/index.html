Dynasty Rookie Risers and Fallers Week 10: Who Moved Since Nov 4

Week 10 dynasty rookie risers and fallers since Nov 4: KC Concepcion Jr. +491, Denzel Boston +343, Jordyn Tyson -1,098, plus market gaps.

As of Nov 11, 2026, Jeremiyah Love is still the top 2026 rookie at 5,599 in 1QB leagues, ahead of Carnell Tate (4,065). KC Concepcion Jr. is the biggest riser since Nov 4, +491 to 2,181. Jordyn Tyson is the biggest faller, -1,098 to 1,614.

By DynastyMath Staff · Published November 11, 2026 · Numbers as of November 11, 2026 · 6 min read

Jordyn Tyson was third in the class a week ago. Now he is seventh. Denzel Boston and KC Concepcion Jr. took the room: Denzel Boston holds third at 2,428, and KC Concepcion Jr. jumped 491 to fourth. At the top, nothing changed: Jeremiyah Love and Carnell Tate still hold the first two spots.

This is the Week 10 rookie board. Values are on a 0 to 10,000 scale as of Nov 11, 2026, and every change compares to Nov 4. 87 first-year players carry a value. Of the 84 that had one on Nov 4, 40 gained, 30 lost and 14 did not move. Full class: dynasty rookie rankings. The tables skip rookies worth less than 250 on both dates, so tiny moves on tiny prices stay out.

KC Concepcion Jr. (CLE). Up 491 (+29.1%) to 2,181, the largest gain in the class by points. He has 9 games, 9.8 PPR points per game and 16.2 over the last three. Experts have him at 2,600 and the Sleeper market at 1,550, so he carries a buy flag in our data.

Denzel Boston (CLE). Up 343 (+16.5%) to 2,428, and he holds third in the class. 9 games, 11.4 PPG, 10.4 over the last three. Experts (2,492) and Sleeper (2,036) sit close together.

Makai Lemon (PHI) and Antonio Williams (WAS). Makai Lemon is up 135 to 1,402 with 14.5 PPG over his last three against 8.2 for the season. Antonio Williams is up 80 to 703 on 7.6 PPG. Makai Lemon is the one with the bigger split between sources: experts 2,334, Sleeper 535.

Jonah Coleman (DEN) and Germie Bernard (PIT). Jonah Coleman is up 190 to 1,908 and Germie Bernard is up 202 to 1,453. Both sit on the sell side of the draft market: Sleeper is far above the experts for each (2,712 against 1,004, and 2,328 against 578).

Jordyn Tyson (NO). Down 1,098 (-40.5%) to 1,614, the biggest drop in the class by points and by percent. He has played 1 game. Experts still have him at 2,693, while the Sleeper draft market sits at 176. One game is a thin sample, so treat the price as unsettled.

Jadarian Price (SEA) and Omar Cooper Jr. (NYJ). Jadarian Price is down 187 to 922, scoring 6.3 per game. Omar Cooper Jr. gave back 134 to 2,004, even though his last three average 19.5 PPG against 12.5 for the season.

What to do with this

  1. Price the one-game rookies carefully. Jordyn Tyson and Fernando Mendoza both have one game played. Their values moved a lot on very little production.
  2. Check the tag. Jeremiyah Love is listed IR and Carnell Tate is listed Questionable. Price that into any offer. Our data has no return dates.
  3. Price a pick against a rookie. A 2027 Early 1st is 4,386 in 1QB leagues (5,220 superflex) and a 2027 Mid 1st is 1,985 (2,317). See the full rookie pick values and run both sides through the trade analyzer.
  4. Rank before you offer. Build your list on the rookie draft board so you know your price before the other manager names theirs.
