Week 10 Fantasy TE Streamers by Projection

Week 10 fantasy TE streamers as of Nov 14, 2026: ten tight ends outside the top 10 by value, ranked by PPR projection with last-3 PPG, adds and injury tags.

As of Nov 14, 2026, the top Week 10 tight end streamer by PPR projection is Travis Kelce at 12.3, ahead of Brenton Strange and Hunter Henry (9.9 each) and Mike Gesicki (9.8). Only Kelce projects above 10 outside the top 10 tight ends by value.

By DynastyMath Staff · Published November 14, 2026 · Numbers as of November 14, 2026 · 5 min read

Streaming tight end is a hunt for 9 or 10 points, and this week the list is short. Here are the Week 10 options outside the top 10 by dynasty value, ordered by PPR projection. Values and projections as of Nov 14, 2026.

Skip the top of the board. George Kittle (15.3), Brock Bowers (15.1) and Trey McBride (15) all project above every tight end below, and all three rank in the top 10 by value. The pool here is the 18 tight ends ranked 11th or lower by value, inside our top 200. 13 of them have a Week 10 projection, and the ten highest are below. Projections come from Sleeper. Check your own waiver wire for who is free.

5 in-range tight ends have no Week 10 projection: T.J. Hockenson, Terrance Ferguson, Dallas Goedert, Jake Ferguson and Eli Stowers. Treat that as missing data, not a zero. Hockenson and Jake Ferguson are listed Out and Terrance Ferguson is listed IR. Goedert and Stowers carry no injury tag, so for them the projection is simply not there.

Travis Kelce tops the pool at 12.3, 2.4 points clear of the next name, with 12.4 per game on the season and 12.5 over his last three, the best recent average of the ten. He is also 37, the oldest of the ten, and has no injury tag. Kelce is the only one of the ten projected for 10 or more.

Brenton Strange and Hunter Henry tie at 9.9. Strange (age 25) averages 9 per game and 7 over the last three. Henry (age 31) is steadier, 8.9 per game and 8.9 lately. Mike Gesicki is a tenth of a point back at 9.8, with 10 per game and 9.4 over his last three.

Pat Freiermuth projects 8.9 off a 9.9 season average, but his last three games average only 6.2. Chig Okonkwo (8.7) is listed Questionable, the only tag among the ten. Kyle Pitts Sr. (8.6) and Juwan Johnson (8.5) round out the middle. AJ Barner (7.7) and Michael Mayer (6.1) are the floor of the list, and neither has a tag.

Who is trending, and who is not

Five of the ten show Sleeper adds over 72 hours. Brenton Strange leads with 222,921 in 72 hours and 51,984 in 24, the most of the ten on both counts. Mike Gesicki is next at 183,516, then Hunter Henry at 101,811. AJ Barner (54,474) and Kyle Pitts Sr. (46,774) follow. Kelce, Freiermuth, Okonkwo, Johnson and Mayer show no 72-hour adds.

Adds are a crowd signal. They tell you where other managers are looking, not who scores. Strange pairs the crowd with a 9.9 projection, but his last-3 average of 7 sits below his 9 season pace. The value is 903, so he is a stream, not a long-term hold.

Juwan Johnson has the widest plus against the last-3 average (+4.1), because his last three games averaged 4.4. Chig Okonkwo has the widest plus against the season pace (+1.4). Michael Mayer is the mirror image: -3.0 against the season pace, the widest minus of the ten, so Sleeper expects him to score well below his own average. Kyle Pitts Sr. is worth 1,276, second only to Kelce among the ten, so he is a hold before a stream.

Travis Kelce has the longest runway: 37.1 points over three weeks, the highest sum of the ten. Hunter Henry, Kyle Pitts Sr. and AJ Barner have projections for only two of the three weeks and no Week 11 number. If you want a multi-week stream, those three come with a missing Week 11 projection.

How to use this

  1. Check Chig Okonkwo on the injury report before kickoff. He is the only one of the ten with a tag.
  2. If two options tie, take the better last-3 average and the clean health tag.
  3. Compare any two in the start/sit tool before you drop a tight end.
  4. Never cut a dynasty piece for a one-week stream. Use the waiver wire for the add, not the cut.
  5. For the rest of the season, see the ROS tight end rankings and the Week 10 start/sit page.

Frequently asked questions

Who is the best tight end streamer in Week 10?

Only Travis Kelce, at 12.3. Brenton Strange and Hunter Henry (9.9 each) and Mike Gesicki (9.8) are the next three.
