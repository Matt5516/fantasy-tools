Week 12 Fantasy Breakouts: Last-3 PPG vs Season PPG

Week 12 fantasy breakouts as of Nov 24, 2026: ten players whose last-3 PPR PPG beats season PPG, plus eight fades and their Week 12 projections.

As of Nov 24, 2026, Jonah Coleman has the biggest jump for Week 12, 24.7 PPR points per game over the last three games against 16.3 for the season (+8.4). Dak Prescott has the highest last-3 average at 29.9. On the fade side, Josh Allen is down 8.6 (24.4 to 15.8).

By DynastyMath Staff · Published November 25, 2026 · Numbers as of November 24, 2026 · 5 min read

A hot three games sells. A projection pays. Here are the Week 12 players whose last-3 PPR average runs ahead of their season average, then the starters going the other way. Numbers as of Nov 24, 2026.

The rules: at least 4 games played, a full 3-game window, and a season average of 8.0 PPR points or better. Anyone listed Out, Doubtful, IR, PUP or suspended is out. We also skipped the 17 names from last week's breakouts piece so you see new ones. That leaves 117 players, 51 above their season pace and 64 below. Ranked by last-3 minus season.

Breakouts the projections do not back

Three risers show the widest distance between the streak and the forecast. Season PPG is in brackets: Jonah Coleman (16.3), Cam Ward (14.8), Dak Prescott (22.2).

  • Jonah Coleman: 24.7 over the last three, 14.5 projected for Week 12, a gap of 10.2.
  • Cam Ward: 22.5 over the last three, 12.8 projected for Week 12, a gap of 9.7.
  • Dak Prescott: 29.9 over the last three, 20.7 projected for Week 12, a gap of 9.2.

That does not make them sells. It means the forecast expects a return toward the season number. If you have to choose between two similar starters, take the one with the higher projection.

Frequently asked questions

Which fantasy players are breaking out in Week 12?

As of Nov 24, 2026, the biggest gaps between last-3 and season PPR average are Jonah Coleman (+8.4), Dak Prescott (+7.7), Cam Ward (+7.7) and Justice Hill (+6.9). The pool is players with at least 4 games and a season average of 8.0 or better, and names from last week's list are skipped.

Is last-3 PPG better than season PPG for start/sit?

Neither alone. Last-3 is a small sample. All ten risers are projected below their last-3 average for Week 12. Use last-3 to break ties between players with similar projections.

Who is cooling off in fantasy football this week?

The biggest drops from season average to last-3 average are Josh Allen (-8.6), Kirk Cousins (-6.8) and Jalen Coker (-5.4). Check the projection column before you bench any of them.

Should I add a player just because their last 3 games are hot?

Check the projection first. Dak Prescott and Bucky Irving share the highest Week 12 projection of the risers at 20.7, and all ten risers are projected below their last-3 pace. Read the Weeks column too: some next-3 sums cover only two weeks.
