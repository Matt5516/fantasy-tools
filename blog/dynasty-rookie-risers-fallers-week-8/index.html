Dynasty Rookie Risers and Fallers Week 8: What Changed Since Week 7

Week 8 dynasty rookie risers and fallers since Oct 20: Omar Cooper Jr. +1,266, Caleb Douglas +711, Germie Bernard -315, plus what a 2027 first costs.

As of Oct 27, 2026, the biggest 2026 rookie riser since Oct 20 is Omar Cooper Jr., up 1,266 to 2,350 (+116.8%), now the fourth-most valuable rookie. The biggest faller is Germie Bernard, down 315 (-23.6%) to 1,017. Carnell Tate still leads the class at 4,296.

By DynastyMath Staff · Published October 27, 2026 · Numbers as of October 27, 2026 · 6 min read

One week changed the rookie board. Omar Cooper Jr. jumped from 10th to 4th. Denzel Boston lost more than a tenth of his value. Carnell Tate and Jeremiyah Love held the top two spots. This is the Week 8 update to our Oct 20 rookie article: same class, new numbers. Values are on a 0 to 10,000 scale as of Oct 27, 2026, and changes compare to Oct 20. 87 first-year players carry a value. Of the 84 that had a value on Oct 20, 19 gained, 49 lost and 16 did not move. The full class lives on the dynasty rookie rankings.

The order at the top held. Carnell Tate is still first at 4,296, down 58 on the week, with 11 PPR points per game over 7 games. Jeremiyah Love is second at 4,256 (+109), but his sources do not agree: the experts have him at 7,736, the Sleeper draft market at 830. He is listed Out and has played 5 games. That is a buy flag, not a guarantee. Jordyn Tyson dropped to 2,952 (-306) with 0 games played and an IR tag, so his number rests on the expert consensus alone.

The big move is fourth place. Omar Cooper Jr. is now ahead of Denzel Boston. He was 10th a week ago.

The risers

Omar Cooper Jr. (NYJ). Up 1,266 to 2,350, the biggest gain in the class. He has played 3 games and averages 16.3 PPR points per game. Three games is a tiny sample. The sources are split: experts at 897, draft market at 1,493, production at 7,939. The market is 66% above the experts, which earns him a sell flag.

Caleb Douglas (MIA). Up 711 (+261.4%) to 983. Also three games, 13.1 PPG. The experts sit at 180, the market at 480. Production is the loud signal here, at 4,131. Treat the jump as a small-sample spike until the games pile up.

Jonah Coleman (DEN). Up 82 (+14.4%) to 652 with 7.9 PPG over 3 games and a Week 8 projection of 10.1. His projection is above his average, which is not true of every riser here.

The fallers

Jordyn Tyson (NO). Down 306 (-9.4%) to 2,952, the second-biggest drop by points. Same story as above: no games, an IR tag and one source.

Denzel Boston (CLE). Down 273 (-11.5%) to 2,107. He has played all 7 games at 11 PPG, but his last three are lower at 7.3. His sources are close together (experts 2,055, market 2,055), so every source prices him in the same place. Teammate KC Concepcion Jr. barely moved (-11, now 1,539) and has the better last three, 10.9 PPG against 9 on the year.

Germie Bernard (PIT). The biggest drop in the class, by points and by percentage among rookies worth 1,000 or more: -315 (-23.6%) to 1,017. His last three games average 15.3 PPG, better than his 8.4 for the season, so the production did not fall. The experts have him at 188 against a market of 1,695. He still carries a sell flag, at a lower price.

Kenyon Sadiq (NYJ). Down 170 (-15.5%) to 930. A tight end with 6.1 PPG and just 1.4 over his last three games, against an expert value of 1,629.

Three ways to use this before your deadline

  1. Sell the spike. A rookie who gained 1,000 points on three games, like Omar Cooper Jr., is priced for his best week. If a manager pays it, take it.
  2. Buy the discount. Run Jeremiyah Love and Makai Lemon through the trade analyzer. Both have expert values far above the draft market. Both carry real risk: Love is listed Out and Lemon has scored 7.1 PPG.
  3. Price picks against players. If a 2027 mid first buys less than a Denzel Boston, decide whether you want a known player or a draft slot.

Frequently asked questions

Who is the top 2026 dynasty rookie in Week 8?

Carnell Tate (WR, TEN) leads at 4,296 in 1QB leagues and 3,190 in superflex, ahead of Jeremiyah Love (4,256) and Jordyn Tyson (2,952), as of Oct 27, 2026.

Which rookie gained the most dynasty value this week?

Omar Cooper Jr. (NYJ) is up 1,266 points since Oct 20, from 1,084 to 2,350. Caleb Douglas (MIA) is next at +711.

Which rookie lost the most dynasty value this week?

Germie Bernard (PIT) lost the most, -315 points (-23.6%) to 1,017. Jordyn Tyson is next at -306, then Denzel Boston at -273.

How much is a 2027 first-round pick worth in dynasty?

As of Oct 27, 2026, an early 2027 first is 4,182 in 1QB (4,913 superflex), a mid first is 1,864 (2,171) and a late first is 858 (1,014).

Are rookie values reliable after three games?

Not very. Omar Cooper Jr. and Caleb Douglas have each played 3 games, and their values moved 1,266 and 711 points in a week. Expect big swings until the samples grow.
