Superflex QB Trade Values Week 10: Who Rose and Who Fell

Superflex QB trade values for Week 10 as of Nov 13, 2026: Tyler Shough +1,571, Jacoby Brissett -2,029 since Nov 7, plus 7 fair trades.

As of Nov 13, 2026, Josh Allen is still the top superflex asset at 9,889 (6,353 in 1QB). Since Nov 7, Tyler Shough rose the most among QBs (+1,571 to 4,522), and Jacoby Brissett fell the most among QBs worth 1,000 or more on Nov 7 (-2,029 to 473). 5 of the top 12 superflex assets are QBs. In 1QB, 0 are.

By DynastyMath Staff · Published November 13, 2026 · Numbers as of November 13, 2026 · 6 min read

The QB board moved in Week 10. Bryce Young, Tyler Shough and C.J. Stroud jumped. Brock Purdy and Patrick Mahomes slid. If you priced a quarterback last week, that price is stale. Values use a 0 to 10,000 scale, as of Nov 13, 2026. Changes compare to Nov 7, the date of last week's list. 1QB value is the price in a one-QB league. Superflex value is the price when a second QB can start. FFC had no dynasty data today, so the draft-market side comes from Sleeper.

Our data does not say why. A riser can be a sell. A faller can be a buy. The price is the point. If you held C.J. Stroud on Nov 7, you held 4,463 of superflex value. Today it is 5,599.

How to use this

  1. Quote the right column. Use the superflex number in superflex leagues and the 1QB number in 1QB leagues.
  2. Check the change since Nov 7. A QB up 1,571 is priced differently than he was a week ago. Counter with today's number.
  3. Check the tag. Status is a label in our data, not a timeline.
  4. Run the deal. The trade analyzer uses the same rule as the table above.

Frequently asked questions

Who is the most valuable QB in superflex in Week 10?

Which QBs moved the most since Nov 7?
