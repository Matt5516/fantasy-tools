Dynasty Value Risers and Fallers, Week 12

Week 12 dynasty value risers and fallers since Nov 16, led by Cam Skattebo (+1,509) and James Cook III (-1,150), plus superflex movers and two fair trades.

Since Nov 16 the biggest 1QB dynasty riser is Cam Skattebo at +1,509 (+58.9%), now 4,069. The biggest faller is James Cook III at -1,150, now 5,692. In superflex, J.J. McCarthy leads at +1,678 and Drew Lock falls most at -1,264. As of Nov 23, 2026.

By DynastyMath Staff · Published November 23, 2026 · Numbers as of November 23, 2026 · 7 min read

One week changes a lot of prices. Here are the biggest dynasty value risers and fallers going into Week 12, in 1QB and in superflex, with age, scoring, last-three PPG and injury status. Values as of Nov 23, 2026, on our 0-10,000 scale. Change is today's value minus the value on Nov 16, the date of our Week 11 risers and fallers.

The rule: players valued at 1,000 or more on either date, ranked by raw points. We can't see why a value moved, so we only report where it went. The draft market is Sleeper alone: FFC had no dynasty data today.

How to use this

  1. Sell the flagged risers. If you own a back or receiver who gained 700 or more with a sell flag, price him in the trade analyzer before the spike fades.
  2. Look at last-three PPG next to the price. A faller with 18 or more over his last three, like James Cook III at 18.5, is a different case from one at 4.7.
  3. Check status first. Drake London is Questionable and A.J. Brown is Out. A tag can move a price again.
  4. Mind the format. Superflex puts quarterbacks like J.J. McCarthy and Drew Lock on the lists, and 1QB barely sees them.
  5. Skip the chase. 10 of last week's 20 names reversed. Compare prices on the live risers and fallers page and the trade value chart.

Frequently asked questions

Who are the biggest dynasty value risers in Week 12?

As of Nov 23, 2026, since Nov 16 in 1QB: Cam Skattebo at +1,509, Bucky Irving at +1,234 and Chase Brown at +1,116. In superflex, J.J. McCarthy leads at +1,678.

Who are the biggest dynasty value fallers in Week 12?
