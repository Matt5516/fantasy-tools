Dynasty RB Trade Values Week 10: Tiers, Movers and Fair Swaps

Dynasty RB trade values for Week 10 as of Nov 14, 2026: top 12 backs in three tiers, what changed since Week 8, and four fair swaps within 0.3%.

As of Nov 14, 2026, Jahmyr Gibbs (9,424), Bijan Robinson (8,556) and Kenneth Walker III (7,923) lead dynasty running backs on 1QB value. Since Week 8 (Nov 1), Jeremiyah Love rose the most among the top 12 (+1,688) and Kyren Williams fell the most (-1,301).

By DynastyMath Staff · Published November 14, 2026 · Numbers as of November 14, 2026 · 6 min read

Gibbs is still alone at the top, but the board under him looks different than it did in Week 8. Bijan Robinson dropped to 8,556, James Cook III jumped 1,366 in a week, and Jeremiyah Love climbed back into the top six. Values as of Nov 14, 2026.

This is the running back market in one place: the top 12 by 1QB value, how each moved since Week 8 (Nov 1) and since last Saturday (Nov 7), and four trades that land within 0.3%. Values run on a 0-10,000 scale. "Flag" is the gap between experts and the draft market. A buy flag means the market sits at least 35% below the experts. A sell flag means at least 35% above. Tier one: Gibbs, then a pair that slipped

Jahmyr Gibbs (9,424) is 868 points clear of Bijan Robinson and scores 26.2 per game, the best of the 12. His last three games average 18.5, down from the season pace, and his value is -31 since Week 8. That is a rounding error.

Bijan Robinson is the bigger story. He fell 676 in the past week to 8,556 and sits -670 since Nov 1. He still scores 20 per game, with 17.2 over his last three. Kenneth Walker III (7,923, age 26) is down 358 since Week 8 and is listed Questionable. These three are the only backs above 7,500. The next back, James Cook III, is 962 points below Walker.

Tier two: five thousand to seven thousand

Four backs live here. James Cook III is the headline: 6,607 in Week 8, 5,595 a week ago, 6,961 now. He is up 1,366 (+24.4%) since Nov 7, the biggest gain of any back worth 1,000 or more. At 27 he scores 18.6 per game and 19.7 over his last three.

Christian McCaffrey is the oldest of the 12 at 30 and sits at 5,845 with a sell flag (the market is 105% above the experts). His last three games average 25.1, the best of the 12. A hot stretch plus a sell flag is a window.

Jeremiyah Love (age 21, the youngest of the 12) is back at 5,756, up 1,688 since Week 8, the biggest gain in the top 12. He is listed IR and has played 6 games at 8.9 per game, the lowest of the 12, with 3.7 over the last three. The price is far ahead of the box score. Ashton Jeanty (age 22) is -757 since Week 8 to 5,508 and still carries a buy flag, with the market 78% below the experts.

Tier three: under five thousand

Jonathan Taylor (4,956) fell 1,104 this week, the second-biggest drop in the top 12, and is -1,127 since Week 8. He carries a buy flag and averages 17.1 per game, but only 11.7 over his last three. Kyren Williams has the biggest drop of any back worth 1,000 or more: -1,363 since Nov 7 to 4,299, and -1,301 since Week 8. No flag, and a last-3 of 13.4 against a 17.7 season pace.

Bucky Irving (4,264, age 24) is -815 since Nov 7, yet his last three games average 24.5, the second-best of the 12. Bhayshul Tuten (4,007, age 23) is the other side: +1,246 since Week 8, the second-biggest gain in the top 12, with 21.2 over his last three. Javonte Williams (4,756) carries a sell flag, the market 143% above the experts.

How to use this

  1. Find your backs in the table and note the flag, the change since Nov 1 and the change since Nov 7.
  2. A sell flag on a back who just rose is the best time to move him. A buy flag on a back who just fell is the time to ask.
  3. Run any offer through the trade analyzer before you send it.
  4. Check the full list on the dynasty RB rankings.
  5. Look at the injury report before you pay for a back who is Questionable or on IR.
