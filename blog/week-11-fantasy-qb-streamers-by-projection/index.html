Week 11 Fantasy QB Streamers by Projection

Week 11 fantasy QB streamers as of Nov 22, 2026: ten quarterbacks ranked 13th to 22nd by PPR projection, with last-3 PPG, adds and three-week outlooks.

As of Nov 22, 2026, the top Week 11 QB streamer by projection is Jared Goff at 17, with the best three-week sum of the ten (56). Cam Ward has the best form, 17 over his last three, at 16.9 projected.

By DynastyMath Staff · Published November 22, 2026 · Numbers as of November 22, 2026 · 5 min read

A QB stream is a one-week bet on a number. Here are the Week 11 options outside the top 12, ranked by projection. Projections and adds as of Nov 22, 2026. Week 11 games are being played today, so a stream only works if your QB's game has not started. Check your kickoff times first.

The rule is simple. Take every quarterback with a Week 11 projection, rank them, and drop the top 12. The streamers are the next ten, ranked 13th through 22nd. 25 quarterbacks have a projection, and none of them is tagged Out or IR. Quarterback projections are identical in PPR, half-PPR and standard, so one number covers every format. Season PPG and last-3 PPG are PPR. Adds are Sleeper adds in 24 and 72 hours.

The case for each name

Jared Goff has the top projection at 17. He averages 19.1, the best season PPG of the ten, and his last three games average 11.6. Sleeper has him 2.1 under his season pace and 5.4 over his recent form. His value is 1,001, the highest of the ten. He has no adds showing.

Cam Ward projects 16.9 with 17 over his last three, the best recent form of the group, and 14 on the season. He is 24. Aaron Rodgers (age 42, the oldest of the ten) projects 16.7 and has 98,296 adds in 72 hours, second of the ten. Justin Herbert projects 16.6 against 12 per game, and his last three average 11.5.

Baker Mayfield is the one to watch carefully. He projects 15.2 against 9.1 per game and 5.3 over his last three, the widest gaps above his own numbers in the pool (+6.1 and +9.9). He has played 6 games and is listed Questionable. A projection does not say whether he plays.

Geno Smith (age 36) projects 16.2 with 10.4 over his last three and 50,104 adds in 72 hours. Tyler Shough projects 16.1 and has 18.1 per game on the season, but 14.5 lately. J.J. McCarthy projects 14.7 against 10.6 per game over 5 games.

The two rookies are the biggest crowd draws and the thinnest samples. Fernando Mendoza has 158,712 adds in 72 hours and 31,992 in 24, the most of the ten on both counts, but he has played 2 games. Joe Milton has 79,324 adds and has played 1. Both project 12.1, and neither has more than two games behind the number.

How to use this

  1. Check your starter's status on the injury report first. Baker Mayfield is the one streamer with a tag.
  2. If you need one week and your QB's game has not started, take the best last-3 with a clean tag. That is Cam Ward.
  3. If you plan to hold for two or three weeks, use the table above and pick a name with a full three-week sum.
  4. Compare any two in the start/sit tool before you cut a QB.
  5. Find the add on the waiver wire and watch the trending adds. The full board is on the Week 11 start/sit page.
