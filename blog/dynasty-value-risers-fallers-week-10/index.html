Dynasty Value Risers and Fallers Week 10: Buy, Sell or Hold

The biggest Week 10 dynasty value risers and fallers, led by Jeremiyah Love (+1,739) and Chase Brown (-1,466), with a buy, sell or hold call on each.

The biggest new dynasty value riser since Nov 2 is Jeremiyah Love at +1,739 (+45.1%), now 5,599. The biggest faller is Chase Brown at -1,466 (-34.9%), now 2,731. 4 of the 10 risers carry a sell flag, and 5 of the 10 fallers are buys, led by Javonte Williams. As of Nov 9, 2026.

By DynastyMath Staff · Published November 9, 2026 · Numbers as of November 9, 2026 · 7 min read

Dynasty prices move every day, and a week is long enough to show who is really climbing. Here are the 10 biggest risers and the 10 biggest fallers going into Week 10, each with a buy, sell or hold call. Values as of Nov 9, 2026, on our 0-10,000 scale, 1QB. Change is today's value minus the value on Nov 2, which is also the date of our Week 9 risers and fallers. One comparison covers both.

The rule: players valued at 1,000 or more on either date, ranked by raw points gained or lost. Anyone we featured in the Week 9 piece sits out the main tables so you get new names. They come back in a table further down. We can't see why a value moved, so we don't guess. We only report where it went.

These 10 added 10,975 points between them. Jeremiyah Love leads at +1,739 (+45.1%), now 5,599 and RB8. He is a 21-year-old rookie listed on IR with 6 games played and 8.9 PPG. The experts have him at 7,381, and Sleeper has no dynasty ADP for him in our data, so there is no market flag either way. He is a hold, not a bet that he plays.

4 of the 10 carry a sell flag. That means the draft market sits at least 35% above the experts. D'Andre Swift is up 1,380 (53.4%) to 3,966. The market has him at 6,593 against 2,144 from the experts, 208% higher. Bryce Young went up 1,053 (88.5%) to 2,243 with a market of 3,664 and experts at 738. Jaylen Warren is the same story: market 4,881, experts 1,667. When the price runs past the experts, you sell into it.

Drew Lock is the percentage leader. He went from 15 to 1,044, +1,029 (+6,860.0%), is listed Questionable and has played 3 games. Sample is thin. The experts sit at 30. Treat it as a spike, not a trend.

The clean risers. Bhayshul Tuten is up 1,223 (41.1%) to 4,197 with no flag, 15.9 PPG on the year and 21.2 over his last three. Jayden Daniels is up 1,056 (38.7%) to 3,782, and in superflex he is worth 6,619. Both are holds: the price moved and the market and experts still agree.

Javonte Williams is the clearest: down 1,178 (24.9%) to 3,559 while averaging 16.9 over his last three. Dak Prescott is down 674 (31.9%) to 1,437 and averaging 20.9 over his last three. Breece Hall has played 4 games, so the sample is small, but the gap is wide: market 349, experts 6,232. Omarion Hampton fell 644 (32.0%) to 1,367, with the market at 397 and the experts at 2,395.

A.J. Brown is listed Out and is down 613 (16.5%). Our data gives a status tag and nothing about a return date, so he is a hold.

Buy, sell or hold: the rule

The rule is mechanical. Sell means the draft market sits at least 35% above the experts. Buy means it sits at least 35% below, or a faller who is not listed Out or on IR is averaging 15 or more PPG over his last three. Hold is everything else, including anyone on IR or listed Out. Across the 20 players in the two tables that comes to 4 sells, 5 buys and 11 holds. No riser gets a buy and no faller gets a sell.

  1. Sell the flagged risers first. If you own D'Andre Swift, Bryce Young or Jaylen Warren, price them now at the trade analyzer.
