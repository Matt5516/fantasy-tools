Week 9 Fantasy Breakouts: Last 3 Games vs Season PPG

Week 9 fantasy breakouts as of Nov 6, 2026: ten players whose last-3 PPR PPG runs well above season PPG, plus eight cold starters and their Week 9 projections.

As of Nov 6, 2026, Tony Pollard has the biggest new jump for Week 9, 24.3 PPR points per game over the last three games against 14.6 for the season (+9.7). Tyler Allgeier is next at +6.1. On the cold side, Bryce Young is down 6.8 (18.7 to 11.9).

By DynastyMath Staff · Published November 6, 2026 · Numbers as of November 6, 2026 · 5 min read

Three games is a trend, not a verdict. It is still the best quick read you get before lineups lock. Here are the Week 9 players whose last-3 PPR average runs ahead of their season average, and the starters going the other way. Numbers as of Nov 6, 2026.

The pool: 157 players with at least 4 games played, a full 3-game last-3 window and a season average of 8.0 PPR points or better. 72 are above their season pace and 85 below. We left out anyone listed Out, IR, PUP or suspended, and skipped the names from the last two breakouts pieces so you get fresh ones. Ranked by last-3 minus season.

How to use this

  1. Start with this week's projection. Last-3 is the tiebreaker, not the verdict.
  2. Open the start/sit tool and compare two similar players side by side.
  3. Read the Weeks column before you trust a next-3 sum. A two-week sum is not comparable with a three-week one.
  4. For the longer view, the rest-of-season rankings blend this week's projection, the last 3 games and the season average.
  5. Check the full Week 9 start/sit page before you lock your lineup, and skip any name with an injury tag until their status is clear.

Frequently asked questions

Which fantasy players are breaking out in Week 9?

As of Nov 6, 2026, the biggest gaps between last-3 and season PPR average are Tony Pollard (+9.7), Tyler Allgeier (+6.1), Joe Burrow (+5.9) and Luther Burden III (+5.4). The pool is players with at least 4 games and a season average of 8.0 or better.

Is last-3 PPG better than season PPG for start/sit?

Neither alone. Last-3 is a small sample, and the Week 9 projection for seven of the eight projected risers here sits below his last-3 average. Use last-3 to break ties between players with similar projections.

Who is cooling off in fantasy football this week?

The biggest drops from season average to last-3 average are Bryce Young (-6.8), Kenneth Walker III (-6.2) and Christian Watson (-5.9). Check the projection column before you bench any of them.

Should I add a player just because his last 3 games are hot?

Check the projection first. Joe Burrow is projected for 21.3 in Week 9, the top number among the risers, and seven of the eight projected risers are below their last-3 pace. Read the Weeks column too: some next-3 sums cover only two weeks.
