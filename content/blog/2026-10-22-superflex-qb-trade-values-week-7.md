## Who gets the biggest superflex premium

The premium is superflex value divided by 1QB value. Among the 24 most valuable superflex QBs, the three biggest are Sam Darnold (4.3x, 438 to 1,877), Michael Penix Jr. (3.6x, 568 to 2,038) and Dak Prescott (3.6x, 1,134 to 4,056). Josh Allen has the smallest premium in the table at 1.5x, since his 1QB value is already the highest of any quarterback.

The lesson: a QB outside the top 12 in 1QB can still cost real value in superflex. Do not judge a QB by 1QB rank alone.
