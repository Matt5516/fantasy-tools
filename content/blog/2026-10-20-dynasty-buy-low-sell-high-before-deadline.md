Most leagues close trades somewhere in the middle of the season, so the weeks before your deadline are when a dynasty roster gets rebuilt or locked in. A player is a **buy-low** when the dynasty draft market prices him at least 35% below the expert consensus, and a **sell-high** when it prices him at least 35% above, for players valued at 1,000 or more. As of Oct 20, 2026, that is 26 buys and 33 sells. Here are the eight most valuable of each, what their production says, and six trades the calculator rates as fair. Values as of Oct 20, 2026.

## How to read a flag

Each value blends four inputs on a 0 to 10,000 scale: DynastyProcess expert consensus (40% weight), Sleeper dynasty ADP (30%), Fantasy Football Calculator ADP (15%) and nflverse production (15%). Fantasy Football Calculator had no dynasty data today, so the other three share its weight. **Experts** is the expert value, **Draft market** is Sleeper ADP on the same scale, and **Gap** is how far the market sits from the experts. A flag is a nudge to look closer, not a trade order. Sources and weights: [how our values work](/how-our-values-work/).

**Cashing in on production.** These are not bad players. [Zay Flowers](/dynasty-value-zay-flowers/) averages 18.9 PPR points per game and his composite is up 1,024 points (16.8%) since Oct 14, to 7,109. The experts sit at 5,249 and the market at 9,184. Selling here is a bet that a price that has risen 16.8% in six days is more likely to fall back than keep climbing, not a claim that he will stop scoring. [Christian Watson](/dynasty-value-christian-watson/) is the same story at 6,535: 19.8 PPG at age 27, but the experts value him at 3,913 against a market price of 8,866.

## A short deadline checklist

1. **Check the format.** These flags use 1QB composite values. In superflex the quarterbacks move: [Caleb Williams](/dynasty-value-caleb-williams/) is 2,998 in 1QB but 6,450 in superflex.
2. **Check the status column** before you pay for a discounted player, and check news on the day.
3. **Prefer gaps that production backs up.** Malik Nabers and Ashton Jeanty combine a big discount with solid scoring; a discount on a player who is not scoring needs a reason.
4. **Remember depth.** A two-for-one that looks even on paper costs you a roster spot; the calculator's discount exists for that reason.
5. **Re-check before the deadline.** The [buy-low and sell-high page](/dynasty-buy-low-sell-high/) and [risers and fallers](/dynasty-value-risers-fallers/) refresh every morning. This article is fixed at Oct 20.
