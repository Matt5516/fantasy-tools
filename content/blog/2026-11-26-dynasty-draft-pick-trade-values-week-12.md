## How to use this

1. **Name the slot and the year.** Early, mid, late and 2028 are four different prices.
2. **Quote your format.** Superflex numbers belong in superflex leagues.
3. **Check the date on the player.** The biggest 1QB riser moved 2,298 points in a week. Price the player on the day you trade.
4. **Count the players above it.** The early 1st has 36 players above it in 1QB. Fewer above a pick means it carries more weight.
5. **Price the exact deal.** Run it through the [dynasty trade analyzer](/trade-analyzer/), browse the [rookie pick values](/rookie-draft-pick-values/), check the [trade value chart](/dynasty-trade-value-chart/) and scout the class on the [rookie draft board](/rookie-draft-board/).
