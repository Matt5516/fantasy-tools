## The risers

**Omar Cooper Jr. (NYJ).** Up 1,266 to 2,350, the biggest gain in the class. He has played 3 games and averages 16.3 PPR points per game. Three games is a tiny sample. The sources are split: experts at 897, draft market at 1,493, production at 7,939. The market is 66% above the experts, which earns him a sell flag.

**Caleb Douglas (MIA).** Up 711 (+261.4%) to 983. Also three games, 13.1 PPG. The experts sit at 180, the market at 480. Production is the loud signal here, at 4,131. Treat the jump as a small-sample spike until the games pile up.

**[Jonah Coleman](/dynasty-value-jonah-coleman/) (DEN).** Up 82 (+14.4%) to 652 with 7.9 PPG over 3 games and a Week 8 projection of 10.1. His projection is above his average, which is not true of every riser here.

## The fallers

**Jordyn Tyson (NO).** Down 306 (-9.4%) to 2,952, the second-biggest drop by points. Same story as above: no games, an IR tag and one source.

**Denzel Boston (CLE).** Down 273 (-11.5%) to 2,107. He has played all 7 games at 11 PPG, but his last three are lower at 7.3. His sources are close together (experts 2,055, market 2,055), so every source prices him in the same place. Teammate [KC Concepcion Jr.](/dynasty-value-kc-concepcion-jr/) barely moved (-11, now 1,539) and has the better last three, 10.9 PPG against 9 on the year.

**Germie Bernard (PIT).** The biggest drop in the class, by points and by percentage among rookies worth 1,000 or more: -315 (-23.6%) to 1,017. His last three games average 15.3 PPG, better than his 8.4 for the season, so the production did not fall. The experts have him at 188 against a market of 1,695. He still carries a sell flag, at a lower price.

**[Kenyon Sadiq](/dynasty-value-kenyon-sadiq/) (NYJ).** Down 170 (-15.5%) to 930. A tight end with 6.1 PPG and just 1.4 over his last three games, against an expert value of 1,629.

## Three ways to use this before your deadline

1. **Sell the spike.** A rookie who gained 1,000 points on three games, like Omar Cooper Jr., is priced for his best week. If a manager pays it, take it.
2. **Buy the discount.** Run Jeremiyah Love and Makai Lemon through the [trade analyzer](/trade-analyzer/). Both have expert values far above the draft market. Both carry real risk: Love is listed Out and Lemon has scored 7.1 PPG.
3. **Price picks against players.** If a 2027 mid first buys less than a Denzel Boston, decide whether you want a known player or a draft slot.
