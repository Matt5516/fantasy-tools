## How to use this

1. **Start with the backed list.** If one fits a hole on your roster, bid on him.
2. **Match the add to your need.** A second quarterback does nothing in a one-quarterback league.
3. **Skip projections under 6.** Heavy adds with a small projection is the pattern to avoid.
4. **Check status before kickoff.** These numbers are from Monday. A status change beats them.
5. **Know your own deadlines.** Waiver and trade deadlines are league settings.
6. **Go past the top 25.** The [full Week 12 waiver wire](/week-12-waiver-wire/) updates daily, and the [waiver wire tool](/waiver-wire/) sorts it against your roster.
