Week 8 is when dynasty trade talks get serious. Here is the board as of Oct 28, 2026: 29 buy-low flags, 33 sell-high flags, and what changed since Oct 20.

A **buy-low** is a player the dynasty draft market prices at least 35% under the expert consensus. A **sell-high** is the reverse: at least 35% over. Only players valued at 1,000 or more count. Values as of Oct 28, 2026.

## How to read a flag

**Experts** is the DynastyProcess consensus. **Draft market** is Sleeper dynasty ADP on the same 0 to 10,000 scale. **Gap** is the market against the experts. **Value** is our blended number, and **Since Oct 20** is how far it moved over the last eight days. Fantasy Football Calculator had no dynasty data, so the market column is Sleeper only. A flag is a reason to look, not a trade order. Weights are in [how our values work](/how-our-values-work/).

## A short Week 8 checklist

1. **Check the format.** These are 1QB values. In superflex the quarterbacks jump: [Caleb Williams](/dynasty-value-caleb-williams/) is 2,752 in 1QB and 5,453 in superflex.
2. **Check the status column** before you pay for a discount.
3. **Back the gap with production.** Malik Nabers pairs a discount with 25.2 PPG over his last three. A discount on a player who isn't scoring needs a reason you can name.
4. **Mind the roster spot.** A two-for-one that looks even costs you a bench slot.
5. **Re-check before your deadline.** The [buy-low and sell-high page](/dynasty-buy-low-sell-high/) refreshes every morning. This article is fixed at Oct 28, 2026.
