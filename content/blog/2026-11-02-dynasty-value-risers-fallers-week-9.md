## Buy, sell or hold: the rule

The rule is mechanical. **Sell** means the draft market sits at least 35% above the experts. **Buy** means it sits at least 35% below, or a healthy faller is averaging 15 or more PPG over his last three. **Hold** is everything else, including anyone listed Out or on IR. Across the 20 players above that comes to 7 sells, 3 buys and 10 holds.

A hold on an injured player is a hold, not a bet that he comes back.
