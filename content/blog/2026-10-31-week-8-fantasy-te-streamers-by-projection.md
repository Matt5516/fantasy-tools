## Who is trending, and who is not

Seven of the ten show Sleeper add activity. Brenton Strange leads with 1,075,761 adds in 72 hours and 190,062 in 24, the most of the ten on both counts. Hunter Henry is next at 318,978 over 72 hours. Kelce, Goedert and Pat Freiermuth show none.

Adds are a crowd signal. They tell you where other managers are looking, not that the player will score. Strange pairs the crowd with the numbers: 10.9 projected, 13.2 over his last three, no injury tag, and a value of 1,195, third highest of the ten behind Kelce and Hockenson.
