## A short Week 10 checklist

1. **Check the format.** These are 1QB values. In superflex the quarterbacks jump, as Patrick Mahomes shows.
2. **Read the status column** before you pay for a discount.
3. **Look at the last-3 average.** A buy with a falling average is a hold, not a bargain.
4. **Compare to Nov 4.** A flag that survived a week of price moves is sturdier than a brand new one.
5. **Re-check before you offer.** These are Nov 10, 2026 values, and values move every day.
