Past points are a record. Projections are a forecast. When the two disagree, you have a trade. Numbers as of Nov 7, 2026.

For each player, we took the average projected PPR points per week over Weeks 9-11 and compared it with his points per game this season. A **buy** is a player whose projection sits above his season average. A **sell** is a player who has been scoring more than he is projected to score. The pool is 32 receivers, running backs and tight ends. The rules are plain: at least 4 games played, a projection for all three weeks, a rest-of-season score of 12 or higher, and no player ruled Out or on IR. Quarterbacks and the top 12 running backs by dynasty value are left out. This is redraft PPR. Dynasty values are a separate question.

"Difference" is the projection per week minus season PPG. A plus means the forecast is higher than what he has scored. The ROS score blends the projection (40%), last-3 PPG (30%) and season PPG (30%).
