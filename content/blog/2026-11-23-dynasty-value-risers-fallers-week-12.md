## How to use this

1. **Sell the flagged risers.** If you own a back or receiver who gained 700 or more with a sell flag, price him in the [trade analyzer](/trade-analyzer/) before the spike fades.
2. **Look at last-three PPG next to the price.** A faller with 18 or more over his last three, like James Cook III at 18.5, is a different case from one at 4.7.
3. **Check status first.** Drake London is Questionable and A.J. Brown is Out. A tag can move a price again.
4. **Mind the format.** Superflex puts quarterbacks like J.J. McCarthy and Drew Lock on the lists, and 1QB barely sees them.
5. **Skip the chase.** 10 of last week's 20 names reversed. Compare prices on the live [risers and fallers page](/dynasty-value-risers-fallers/) and the [trade value chart](/dynasty-trade-value-chart/).
