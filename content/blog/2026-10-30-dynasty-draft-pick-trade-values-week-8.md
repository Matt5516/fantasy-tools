## Superflex: who pays more

Every pick costs more in superflex. The mid 1st gets the smallest bump of the four firsts, +16%. The late 1st gets the biggest, +18%. The late 2nd climbs +23%, the largest jump on the board.

Quarterbacks show the gap. Bryce Young is 1,664 in 1QB and 4,854 in superflex. Jordan Love is 1,641 and 5,000. In superflex both land next to the early 1st (4,913). In 1QB both cost far less than that pick. Same player, two very different asks.

## How to use this

1. **Name the slot and the year.** Early, mid, late and 2028 are four different prices.
2. **Quote your format.** The superflex number is the one to use in a superflex league.
3. **Pay for the second piece at a discount.** Two picks are worth less than their sum.
4. **Shop the position you need.** Use the position table to see which pick covers a QB, RB, WR or TE.
5. **Check the exact pick.** Run it through the [dynasty trade analyzer](/trade-analyzer/), browse the [rookie pick values](/rookie-draft-pick-values/) and see the class on the [rookie draft board](/rookie-draft-board/).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a 2027 1st round pick worth in dynasty?

As of Oct 29, 2026, a 2027 early 1st is 4,182 in 1QB and 4,913 in superflex. A mid 1st is 1,864 and 2,171. A late 1st is 858 and 1,014.

### How much more is a pick worth in superflex?

Every pick is higher. The four firsts range from +16% (mid 2027 1st) to +18% (late 2027 1st). The late 2027 2nd is +23%.

### How many 2nd round picks equal a 1st?

Not as many as it sounds, because of the depth discount. In raw value an early 1st is 10.0 early 2nds, a mid 1st 4.5 and a late 1st 2.1. Use the trade analyzer for the discounted total.

### Is a 2028 1st worth more than a late 2027 1st?

Yes. A 2028 1st is 1,507 in 1QB and 1,770 in superflex, against 858 and 1,014 for a 2027 late 1st. It is below a mid 2027 1st (1,864 and 2,171).

### Where do these pick prices come from?

They use the same expert and draft-market values that price players. Sleeper dynasty drafts supplied market data as of Oct 29, 2026; Fantasy Football Calculator had no dynasty data that day.
