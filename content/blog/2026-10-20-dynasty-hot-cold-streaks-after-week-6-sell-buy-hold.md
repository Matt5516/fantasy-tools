Three games is a sample, not a verdict. But your league-mates trade on it anyway. So here is the question: who is scoring far above or below his season pace, and does his dynasty value agree?

The rule: players valued at 1,000 or more, with at least 4 games played and a full last-three window. That leaves 99 players. The gap is last-3 PPG minus season PPG in PPR. Season PPG includes those three games, so the real swing is bigger than the gap. Values as of Oct 20, 2026.

## The pattern: heaters get paid, slumps don't get punished

The eight hottest players have a median value change of +14.8% since Oct 14. The eight coldest have a median of +4.5%. The market rewards a streak fast. It barely notices a slump. That is the opening on both sides: sell the heaters while the price is up, and buy the slumpers who are still scoring.

The calls follow three rules. A hot player whose value is up 10% or more is a **sell-high**. A hot player below that is a **hold**. A cold player still averaging 12 or more over his last three, with value up no more than 10%, is a **buy-low**. A cold player under 12 is a **wait**.

## How to use this

1. **Check the price first.** A hot streak with a flat price is a hold. A hot streak with a price up 10% or more is a sale.
2. **Buy slumps that still score.** A cold player at 12 or more per game is not broken.
3. **Skip the true dead.** Kenyon Sadiq is the one on the cold list with a last-three average under 5.
4. **Mind the sample.** Three games is three games. Players with a status tag, like Ollie Gordon (Questionable), need a news check.
5. **Re-check daily.** The [buy-low and sell-high page](/dynasty-buy-low-sell-high/) and [risers and fallers](/dynasty-value-risers-fallers/) update every morning. This article does not.

### How many games make a streak?

Three here. Our window is the last three games, with at least 4 games played and a value of 1,000 or more. That is a small sample, so treat each call as a price check, not a prediction.
