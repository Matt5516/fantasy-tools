## Who is trending, and who is not

Five of the ten show Sleeper adds over 72 hours. Brenton Strange leads with 222,921 in 72 hours and 51,984 in 24, the most of the ten on both counts. Mike Gesicki is next at 183,516, then Hunter Henry at 101,811. AJ Barner (54,474) and Kyle Pitts Sr. (46,774) follow. Kelce, Freiermuth, Okonkwo, Johnson and Mayer show no 72-hour adds.

Adds are a crowd signal. They tell you where other managers are looking, not who scores. Strange pairs the crowd with a 9.9 projection, but his last-3 average of 7 sits below his 9 season pace. The value is 903, so he is a stream, not a long-term hold.
