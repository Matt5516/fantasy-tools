## How to use this

1. **Open with the backed list.** If one fits your roster hole, bid on him.
2. **Match the add to your need.** A second quarterback does nothing in a one-quarterback league.
3. **Skip anyone whose projection is under 6.** A heavy add count with a low projection is the pattern to avoid.
4. **Check status before kickoff.** These numbers are from Monday. A status change beats them.
5. **Go past the top 25.** The [full Week 10 waiver wire](/week-10-waiver-wire/) updates daily, and the [waiver wire tool](/waiver-wire/) sorts it against your own roster. For the weeks ahead, see the [rest-of-season rankings](/rest-of-season-rankings/).
