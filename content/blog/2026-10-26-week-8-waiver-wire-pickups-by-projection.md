## How to use this

1. **Open with the backed list.** If one fits your roster hole, bid on him.
2. **Match the add to your need.** A second quarterback does nothing in a one-quarterback league.
3. **Skip anyone whose projection is under 6.** A heavy add count with a low projection is the pattern to avoid.
4. **Check status on Sunday.** These numbers are from Monday. A status change beats them.
5. **Rank the rest.** The [full Week 8 waiver wire](/week-8-waiver-wire/) and the [trending adds page](/waiver-wire-trending/) update daily. The [waiver wire tool](/waiver-wire/) sorts it against your own roster.
