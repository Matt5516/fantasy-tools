Trending adds by position, with projections and a quick note on each player.

The most-added fantasy players heading into Week 8 are Elijah Mitchell (RB, PHI; 615,357 adds in 24 hours), Jaydon Blue (RB) and Devin Singletary (RB). Counts are Sleeper leagues that added the player in the last 24 hours, as of October 30, 2026.

Trending adds show where the crowd is moving, not who is free in your league or who will produce. The pace column compares the last 24 hours of adds with the average day of the last 72 hours: Rising means today’s adds are at least 25% above that average, Cooling at least 25% below. “Next 3 wks” adds Sleeper’s projections for Weeks 8 to 10; bye weeks and weeks without a projection count as nothing, so a short total can just mean a bye.
