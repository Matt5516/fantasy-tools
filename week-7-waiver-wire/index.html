Trending adds show where the crowd is moving, not who is free in your league or who will produce. The pace column compares the last 24 hours of adds with the average day of the last 72 hours: Rising means today’s adds are at least 25% above that average, Cooling at least 25% below. “Next 3 wks” adds Sleeper’s projections for Weeks 7 to 9; bye weeks and weeks without a projection count as nothing, so a short total can just mean a bye.
