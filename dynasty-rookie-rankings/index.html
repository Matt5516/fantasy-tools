Frequently asked questions

Who is the top 2026 rookie in dynasty right now?

Carnell Tate (WR, TEN) has the highest composite dynasty value among 2026 rookies at 4,354 in 1QB leagues and 3,250 in superflex, followed by Jeremiyah Love (4,147) and Jordyn Tyson (3,258), as of October 20, 2026.

How are these dynasty rookie rankings made?

They are ordered by our composite dynasty value (expert consensus, Sleeper and Fantasy Football Calculator ADP, and nflverse production), limited to players in their first NFL season. Pre-draft grades and college scouting are not part of the number.

Is there a 2027 rookie class ranking?

Not yet; there is no pro data to value that class honestly. What does exist is the market price of 2027 rookie picks, shown below, which is how most dynasty leagues trade that class today.

Can I use this for a startup or rookie draft?

Yes. The rookie draft board tool uses the same values and lets you tick players off as they are drafted.
