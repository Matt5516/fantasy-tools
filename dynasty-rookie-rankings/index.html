Frequently asked questions

Who is the top 2026 rookie in dynasty right now?

Jeremiyah Love (RB, ARI) has the highest composite dynasty value among 2026 rookies at 5,756 in 1QB leagues and 4,522 in superflex, followed by Carnell Tate (4,181) and Denzel Boston (2,829), as of November 13, 2026.

How are these dynasty rookie rankings made?

By dynasty value (expert consensus, Sleeper and Fantasy Football Calculator ADP, and nflverse production), first-year players only. Pre-draft grades and college scouting aren’t in the number.

Is there a 2027 rookie class ranking?

Not yet. That class has no pro data to value. The market price of 2027 rookie picks is below, and that is how most dynasty leagues trade the class.

Can I use this for a startup or rookie draft?

Yes. The rookie draft board tool uses the same values and lets you tick players off as they are drafted.
