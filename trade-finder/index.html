    FAQ

    What counts as a fair trade here?

    Both sides land within 5% of each other after the same depth discount the trade analyzer uses. The best piece counts in full and extra pieces count less, so a 2-for-1 needs more total value than a 1-for-1.

    Why do some 2-for-1 packages add up to so much?

    Roster spots are limited. The second player in a package counts at 75% of his value, so two mid-value players have to add up to more than the one star you're moving.

    Does superflex change the list?

    Yes. Switch to Superflex and quarterbacks jump in value, so the packages change. Pick the format your league plays before you shop a deal.
