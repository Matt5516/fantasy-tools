Frequently asked questions

What is a dynasty buy-low or sell-high target?

A buy-low target is a player whose price in the draft market is well below what expert rankings say he is worth; a sell-high is the reverse. Here, “market” is Sleeper (and Fantasy Football Calculator when available) dynasty ADP and “experts” is the DynastyProcess consensus, and a flag needs a gap of at least 35% with a composite value of 1,000 or more.

Which dynasty players are the best buy-low targets right now?

By composite value, the top flagged buys as of October 20, 2026 are Malik Nabers (6,437), Ashton Jeanty (6,292), Breece Hall (4,496), Jeremiyah Love (4,147), George Pickens (4,000).

Which dynasty players are the best sell-high candidates right now?

By composite value, the top flagged sells as of October 20, 2026 are Zay Flowers (7,109), Christian Watson (6,535), Kyren Williams (5,829), Christian McCaffrey (5,205), Javonte Williams (4,579).

Does a buy or sell flag mean I should make the trade?

No. It is a prompt to look closer. The market can be right and the experts wrong, and age, role, injuries and your own roster and scoring settings are not part of the flag.

When is the fantasy trade deadline?

It is a league setting chosen by your commissioner, so check your league’s rules; many leagues close trades in the middle of the season. These tables update daily, so you can re-check before your deadline.
